# A contact is logged ONE way, from every surface: `logContact(entry)` (main.js) + `lib/qso-expand.js`

Recorded 2026-10-19. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**A contact is logged ONE way, from every surface: `logContact(entry)` (main.js) + `lib/qso-expand.js`** (2026-10-19, logging parity audit after W9TEF found three gaps in one window). A surface sends what the operator ENTERED — comma lists of callsigns (multi-op) and of refs (n-fer) welcome — via IPC `log-contact` (desktop forms), `log-qso` (ECHOCAT; capability `log-contact` tells the app it may send one contact) or a direct call (JTCAT `jtcatAutoLog`, which the device's `jtcat-log-qso` now also uses; the WSJT-X bridge). `expandContact` decides the records: callsign × their ref (`sig`+`sigInfo` list, or `hunted:[{sig,ref}]` where each record keeps only its own program field) × my activation ref (the LIVE activation via `liveActivationMyRefs()` — `activationActive || appMode==='activator'`; `entry.mySig` = exactly that one ref; `entry.activation:false` = none, the Logbook's New QSO); only the first record re-spots and keeps the caller uuid; each CALLSIGN's first record is forwarded (external logbook/QRZ/Club Log), the rest `skipLogbookForward`; a park contact is logged at the park (`parkInfoFor` = parks DB, else the live spot — the DB is the operator's own program only) or the operator-picked `parkState`, NEVER a home QTH, `gridHeard` (FT8/JS8 decode) beats the park grid; callsign 1 keeps what the caller typed, every callsign gets its own QRZ name/country; blank RST defaults by mode (59/599/-00); `qsoAt` is the time contract (the web offline queue keeps it). saveQsoRecord writes one finished record (Log as, tags, events, uploads) and hands a comma list it receives to logContact. `announceActivationContact` feeds the Act screen + device session list for contacts logged elsewhere. **Never build records in a surface again** — no saveQso loops, no `skipLogbookForward`, no mySig cross-products: `test/logging-parity-test.js` fails; the rules are `test/qso-expand-test.js`. Per-surface by design: the multi-state park prompt, dupe warnings, the Log-as chooser, re-spot choice/text. Mobile handoff: potacat-app `docs/desktop-handoffs/log-contact.md`.
