# Flex Direct makes its own slice when the radio restores none

Recorded 2026-10-17. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**Flex Direct makes its own slice when the radio restores none** (K3SBP 2026-10-17: the 8600 had persisted an EMPTY layout against POTACAT's client_id — a ghost registration under the same id was dropped by the radio and its slice went with it — so every launch logged "Flex Direct active" and "RADIO LINK LOST" a second apart and refused every tune, with no reason in the log). `client gui` only lets the radio restore what it persisted; `canTune` in self-host also needs a slice tagged with our handle. So `SmartSdrClient._armSelfSlice()` runs on the gui ack and on `slice-lost` (our slice `removed`/`in_use=0`, which until now left `_ourSliceIndex` pointing at a dead index and every later command answering "Slice receiver (N) not in use"): after `_selfSliceGraceMs` (4 s) with no slice, `_ensureSelfSlice()` sends `slice create freq= mode=` with NO `pan=` — **verified on the 8600/v4: the radio builds the panadapter itself and tags both with our handle**, so the ordinary self-host adoption in `_parseSliceStatus` picks it up (`createSlice({forSelf:true})` adopts instead of excluding — the exclusion is the rule for slices made for ANOTHER app). Rate-limited (`_selfSliceRetryMs` 30 s); a refusal is `slice-create-failed` + a log line + toast, never a retry storm. main.js supplies the dial via `setSliceSupplier(flexSelfSliceWanted)`: this session's dial, else `settings.flexLastSlice {hz, mode}` (written from the self-hosted slice's own frequency/mode events, 3 s debounce — the radio's persistence is exactly the thing that can come back empty, so POTACAT remembers the dial itself), else the library's 14.074 USB. **The link pill lights on self-host `slice-ready`, not on `gui-ready`** (registration cannot tune; the slice can), and the LOST line names the real cause on a Flex. A bound helper can do the same by hand (`client bind client_id=<id>` then `slice create`). Tests: `test/smartsdr-self-slice-test.js`.
