# A courtesy 73/RR73 leg ends when its message has been TRANSMITTED, never after a count of decode cycles

Recorded 2026-10-15. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**A courtesy 73/RR73 leg ends when its message has been TRANSMITTED, never after a count of decode cycles** (IK4IDF 2026-10-15: "not sending the 73, calling CQ or hunting POTA; I have to double-click the report"). Decode events fire every period, our own TX period included, so the old "wait one cycle, then tear down" assumed the courtesy went out in the very next slot; a decode landing after that slot's TX had started (slow PC + multi-pass decoder, FT4 with no wait-for-decode path) sent the OLD message once more and closed the QSO with the 73 never sent. `_courtesyPending()` in `lib/jtcat-state-machine.js` holds the leg (TX armed, message intact) until main's tx-start dispatch stamps `q._txSentMsg` (`jtcatNoteTxSent(data.message)` beside the `_lastTxPeriodKey` try stamp, single- and multi-slice, after every TX-block guard) and `engine._txActive` is false; bounded by `COURTESY_MAX_WAIT_CYCLES` (4) so a TX that can never go out still closes. `jtcatHandleRetryStall` returns early on courtesy phases. Tests: jtcat-test.js "Courtesy leg — ends when the 73/RR73 has actually been transmitted". Log tell: `courtesy 73 wait (not transmitted yet — the last TX carried the previous message; cycle N of 4)`.
