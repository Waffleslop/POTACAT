# The SSTV decoder normalises its input; every amplitude gate in it is calibrated for -25 to -3 dBFS

Recorded 2026-09-18. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**The SSTV decoder normalises its input; every amplitude gate in it is calibrated for -25 to -3 dBFS** (WB8IMY 2026-09-18: a strong image at ~-40 dBFS sat in IDLE for the whole transmission, `e19=0.00x`, while the auto-ranging waterfall painted it bright and the NO RX AUDIO badge only fires below -80 dBFS; MMSSTV and WSJT-X normalise, so his audio worked there). `lib/sstv-worker.js _runDsp`: the Hilbert/phase path (pixels) runs on the RAW sample; only the tone envelopes and `mag` see the gain. The tracker is a 20 ms BLOCK MEDIAN of the analytic magnitude (a crash shorter than half a block cannot move it; noise can only raise it), moves only at block boundaries (per-sample decay left the frozen gain anywhere in 1.000–1.010 and cost the noisy PSNR tiers 19 dB), and the gain is `max(1, INPUT_TARGET·INPUT_REF_MAG/peak)`: **boost only, to -6 dBFS, never touching anything above** — so the full-scale PSNR matrix is byte-identical — and **frozen from the VIS start bit to the end of the image** (a gain step inside a 9 ms sync pulse moves the sync argmax; free-running cost Martin 1 14 dB). Why not full scale as the target: `_envSawLow` needs the 1200 Hz envelope under 0.02 between syncs and a full-scale 2300 Hz pixel leaks 0.018, so at 0 dBFS WHICH lines arm turns on 0.3% of level (the +2000 ppm martin1 cell moved 10 dB) — never "fix" that edge by retuning the target. `INPUT_REF_MAG` is measured at load through the same convolution (closed-form |H(1900)| is 0.3% off). Decode-log IDLE lines now carry `in=-41dBFS gain=+35dB` (raw peak vs full scale); the pop-out shows the level next to Audio In and an amber `RX AUDIO LOW` badge under -50 dBFS. Tests: `test/sstv-input-level-test.js` (same picture at 0/-40/-50/-60 dBFS, gain exactly 1 above -6 dBFS, crash immunity, silence stays IDLE) + the untouched PSNR matrix.
