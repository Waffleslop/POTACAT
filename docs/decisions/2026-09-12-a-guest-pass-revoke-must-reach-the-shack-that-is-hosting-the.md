# A Guest Pass revoke must reach the shack that is hosting the guest

Recorded 2026-09-12. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**A Guest Pass revoke must reach the shack that is hosting the guest** (2026-09-12). The cloud's `DELETE /v1/passes/:code` flips the row and closes `pass_sessions`, but nothing pushes that to the desktop, and `PassEnforcement.endPass()` was reached only by natural expiry, the owner's banner button, 'disconnected' and 'superseded' — so Share My Rig's Revoke (which promises "kicked from the rig immediately") left a connected guest on the rig until the pass expired. Two paths now end it, both in `lib/pass-enforcement.js`: the desktop's own revoke calls `endPassIfCode(code,'revoked')` through the `onPassRevoked` ctx hook (`lib/cloud-ipc.js` `passes-revoke` → main.js), and a revoke made anywhere else (mobile device, a second desktop, admin) is caught by `_revalidate()`, a 60 s GET of the live pass — **404 is the only evidence of a revoke** (the cloud answers one 404 for missing/expired/revoked/unstarted alike, and a live session cannot be the other three); 429/5xx/no-network are not evidence and the session stays up, a shorter `expires_at` is adopted, a longer one ignored. The poll stops with the session, so the one 404 is never repeated at the cloud's failure limiter. 'ended' → `broadcastPassEnded(reason, code)` in remote-server.js then kicks the pass-authenticated WS clients AND purges `_passGateSessions` for that code, which is what turns a browser guest's next request into the connect page (`/?notice=pass-ended`). Tests: `test/guest-pass-test.js` (PassEnforcement section, stubbed fetch, 20 ms poll).
