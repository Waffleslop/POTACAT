# "Log as" another operator: one QSO into another profile's logbook, with THEIR settings, without switching

Recorded 2026-10-16. Moved verbatim from the desktop CLAUDE.md on 2026-11-09; the original is archived in the potacat workspace at docs/history/CLAUDE-desktop-2026-11-09.md.

**"Log as" another operator: one QSO into another profile's logbook, with THEIR settings, without switching** (KD9WI 2026-10-16: two hams at one shack PC taking turns). `lib/log-as.js` (pure) resolves `qsoData.logAs` against `listProfiles()`; `saveQsoRecord` reads identity, logbook file and uploads from `ops` (= `operatorSettingsFor(call)`: their profile file under this machine's global keys) — OPERATOR/STATION_CALLSIGN forced to that call, their `adifLogPath`, their QRZ key (`sendToQrzLogbook(qsoData, ops)`), their logbook forward (`forwardToLogbook(qsoData, ops)`), journaled under THEIR cloud owner (`cloudIpc.recordLogChangesFor(ownerIdFor(ops))`, parks until they sign in). Every active-operator cache is skipped under `if (!logAs)` (worked QSOs/parks, events, contest history, Logbook window). Unknown call = refused, never written elsewhere. Surfaces: Log QSO dialog (re-read on open, resets to active; Save button says "Save for W9XYZ"), Log pop-out (own preload `profilesList`), ECHOCAT Web Log tab/sheet/quick log (ONE choice per device). Wire: S2C `operators {active, profiles}` hydrated at connect + after add/archive, C2S `log-qso.logAs`, capability `log-as`; **a Guest Pass never receives the list and its logAs is ignored**. Deliberately NOT on JTCAT auto-log or the Activation logger/quick log (one operator's activation). Mobile handoff: potacat-app `docs/desktop-handoffs/log-as-operator.md`. Log tell: `[QSO] Logged N0OTS for W9XYZ: their logbook, callsign, QRZ and Cloud settings`. Tests: `test/log-as-test.js`.
