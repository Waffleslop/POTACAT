    APRS settings

    iGate

    Each packet your radio hears goes to APRS-IS under your callsign with -10, so it shows on APRS maps as heard by you. It applies whenever POTACAT listens to APRS on your radio, here or in Idle Mode. Nothing from the internet is ever sent on the air.

    My APRS SSID

    Position

    Your position comes from the grid square in Settings, or a point you pick on the map. POTACAT on a computer has no GPS.
