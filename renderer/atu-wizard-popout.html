Fill the radio's antenna tuner memories

POTACAT tunes to every frequency you use, reads the SWR, lets the radio's tuner find a match and stores it in the radio. Afterwards a band change recalls the match instead of running a tune cycle. The radio transmits a low-power carrier for about a second at each point.

Bands and modes

Tick the bands and modes you use. The plan keeps 3 kHz inside every edge of your license class and never transmits outside it.
