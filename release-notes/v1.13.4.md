**Headline:** POTACAT can share a radio with your logger or fldigi through flrig, so the program that already holds your rig keeps it and POTACAT still tunes and transmits. Radios whose data port is silent in USB and LSB, such as the Xiegu G90, can now carry voice over ECHOCAT. Contacts you log in N3FJP on its own can come into your POTACAT log.

## Sharing a radio through flrig

- **New connection type: FLRig (a shared radio).** Settings > My Rigs, add or edit a rig, and choose **FLRig (a shared radio)**. Enter flrig's address and server port (12345 by default; in flrig it is Config > Setup > Xcvr), pick your Radio Model for the right bands and power limits, and press **Test Connection**. The welcome wizard offers it too.
- **What works through flrig:** tuning, mode changes (POTACAT uses your radio's own mode names, such as DATA-U on a Yaesu or USB-D on an Icom or Kenwood), PTT, the S-meter, SWR and power meters, RF power, RF gain, AF level, VFO A/B, split and the tuner start. FT8, SSTV and voice from ECHOCAT all key the radio through flrig.
- **What stays in flrig:** filter width, the noise blanker, CW keying over the radio's CAT port and Custom CAT commands. A CW paddle on the phone says it cannot key CW over this link; CW text and a dedicated CW Key Port still work.
- **Ham2K Polo users:** Polo's own rigctld gives other programs frequency and mode but cannot key the radio. Run flrig as the program that holds the radio and point Polo and POTACAT at it. Thanks KM4CFT.

## Radios

- **Voice in DATA mode.** The rig editor has a new checkbox, **Voice in DATA mode (USB-D / LSB-D)**. With it ticked, POTACAT keeps the radio in USB-D or LSB-D for voice, so receive audio reaches your sound card interface and ECHOCAT. Contacts are still logged as SSB. The radio's own microphone is off in these modes. For the Xiegu G90 and other radios with a silent data port in USB and LSB.
- **Xiegu G90** is set up through Hamlib when you pick it as the Radio Model, which is how it connects reliably.
- **A rigctld server that cannot key the radio is named.** If the program serving rigctld refuses PTT, the log and a notice in the window now say transmit will not work over that link, instead of transmitting nothing in silence. Thanks KM4CFT.
- **Linux /dev/serial/by-id ports** are no longer reported as "not there" forever. Thanks KM4CFT.
- **Flex:** the JTCAT setting "Switch radio to DATA mode" is hidden on a Flex, where it does nothing.

## Logging

- **Bring in contacts from N3FJP.** Settings > Logging > Send Data to Logbook, Logbook N3FJP: tick **Also bring in contacts I log in N3FJP on its own**. While N3FJP is open, POTACAT reads its log every few minutes and adds contacts it does not have yet to your POTACAT log and POTACAT Cloud. Contacts it already has, including the ones POTACAT sent to N3FJP, are skipped. Thanks W6ING.
- **Activation panel frequency.** A frequency typed in kHz into the MHz box, such as 7182, is now read as 7.182 MHz. Contacts saved that way before are repaired when the log loads, so POTA accepts the upload.

## ECHOCAT

- **A voice macro recorded on the phone is confirmed** by the shack, and every connected device sees the new recording. Deleting one from the phone removes it everywhere, and all 25 slots can be played from the phone.
