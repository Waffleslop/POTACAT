**Headline:** Modern Icoms now show their own band scope live in POTACAT, on your phone and behind the spots, thanks to the owners who recorded theirs. The Band Scope window grows proper scales, reference levels, passband and licence shading, and you can tune by dragging. Yaesu CW macros send Morse again instead of a steady carrier, and the WSPR beacon no longer turns some radios up to full power.

## Band scope

- **The Icom's own scope, live.** On an IC-7300, IC-7300 MK II, IC-705, IC-9700, IC-905, IC-7610, IC-7851 or IC-7760, More > Band Scope shows the radio's real scope over the CI-V link POTACAT already uses (USB, CI-V over TCP or Icom Network). Center, Fixed and Scroll modes are all drawn; spots sit on it and a click tunes. It also appears under the FT8 waterfall, in ECHOCAT Web and behind the spots in Bandspread. Over USB the radio sends its scope only at 115200 baud: set CI-V USB Baud Rate to 115200 on the radio and Baud rate 115200 in Settings > My Rigs. Thanks KW4FM, W0EGR, NJ2DX and AE5JE for the recordings.
- **A radio with no scope over CI-V is told so.** An Icom that answers every scope command with "not supported" (the IC-7410, for one) is told it has no band scope POTACAT can show, rather than to check its CI-V address.
- **Scales and levels.** A frequency scale, a level scale, a cursor readout, Top and Bottom levels (drag the level scale to move them), Averaging, and Peak hold with a choice of decay, under the gear in the Band Scope window.
- **What you are on, drawn on the scope.** The passband, the split TX frequency, the band edges, the digital-mode dials and your dial. **Shade outside my privileges** hatches what your licence class (Settings) may not use.
- **Tune with the mouse.** Drag the passband to tune, drag the spectrum and tune on release, the wheel steps the dial by your tuning step, Ctrl+wheel, Page Up / Page Down or a pinch zooms, the arrow keys step, and a double-click on a spot tunes to it. The VFO window and the scope share one tuning step.
- **The scope behind the spots.** Bandspread draws the radio's scope as a faint trace behind the spots wherever the scope covers the band; the **Scope** button turns it off.

## Radios

- **Yaesu CW macros send Morse, not a carrier.** On the FTX-1, FT-710 and other recent Yaesus, POTACAT keyed the radio before handing it the text, and in CW that put out a steady carrier. The radio now keys itself from the text through break-in, the way Hamlib sends it, on a serial link and through rigctld alike. Turn BK-IN on; the log says so if it is off. An FT-710 with no CW Key Port sends the text through the radio's keyer memory 1. Thanks W2XYZ and W3MDT.
- **FTX-1 preamp steps.** IPO, AMP1 and AMP2 on HF and 6 m, on and off on 2 m and 70 cm, set on the band you are on. Thanks N0KAH.
- **Icom voice over the LAN can stay in USB / LSB.** For an Icom on Icom Network whose DATA OFF MOD is LAN, tick **Voice over the LAN stays in USB / LSB** in the rig editor: voice from your phone or the VFO window no longer switches the radio to a DATA mode, so the speech compressor stays on. Thanks EA1BZN.
- **Receive level for your devices.** Set how loud the radio's audio reaches your phone or browser at the shack: **Receive level to ECHOCAT devices** in the rig editor (Settings > My Rigs), or **RX Level** in ECHOCAT Web.
- **Flex Direct on a PC with no microphone** now starts the audio bridge for your phone. Thanks W5ORC.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **The WSPR beacon's power cap no longer means full power.** On a radio connected through rigctld, the 1 W cap was sent as 100%: a KX3 set to 20 W beaconed at 110 W through its amplifier, and a G90 set to 5 W went to 20 W. The cap now reaches the radio as 1 W. Thanks WZ1H.

## Presets

- **NB and DNR levels show your radio's real range** in the preset editor: 0 to 10 on an FTX-1, not 0-100 and 0-15. Thanks N0KAH.
- **A preamp step in a preset.** On a radio with preamp steps, a preset holds IPO, AMP1 or AMP2 instead of on or off. Thanks N0KAH.

## Logging

- **A contact is never logged without a mode.** A contact sent with no mode takes your radio's voice or CW mode. When the radio is in a data mode, or there is no radio, POTACAT asks you to pick the mode instead of saving a contact that pota.app would refuse. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Your grid goes to N3FJP and your other loggers** with each contact made from home, as MY_GRIDSQUARE. POTACAT's own log and your LoTW uploads stay as they are. Thanks AE5JE.
- **Logs from QLog that name the park only as POTA_REF** import with the park, and the Logbook shows it for logs imported before. Thanks KC9FOZ.

## Themes

- **The ECHOCAT themes on your desktop.** Settings > Display > Theme: POTACAT Dark, Midnight, Acid Burn and POTACAT Light, or Classic for the light and dark modes you have now. Every window repaints when you change it.

## Other changes

- **The Rig panel's Presets tab** is no longer cut off at the right edge of the window. Thanks K8IKO.
- **The map's credit line** no longer draws over POTA Stats and the event board. Thanks KQ4SUB.
- **The VFO window's SDR menu** stays inside the window and scrolls, so a long list can be reached from the top.
- **CW on a Mac stays even when POTACAT is in the background:** macOS is kept from slowing POTACAT's timers while it keys CW on a CW Key Port or from a paddle. Thanks N8JMS.
- **ECHOCAT Web's Cloud sign-in** asks for Email or callsign, as the desktop does. Thanks KL4XQ.
