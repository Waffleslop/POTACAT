**Headline:** Winlink now works with your own VARA HF and VARA FM, and POTACAT sets VARA up for you in one click. Also: a Worked All States view with worked and confirmed states and countries tinted on every map, rig profiles that switch your radio's settings by mode and band, and a long list of radio and ECHOCAT fixes.

## Winlink with VARA

- **VARA HF and VARA FM, your own copy (Windows).** Settings > Station > Winlink (email over radio): tick **Use VARA HF** or **Use VARA FM**, then **Set up VARA for me**. POTACAT configures VARA for the active radio (its sound card, PTT, the port), starts it, and checks it is answering. Nothing of VARA is installed or downloaded by POTACAT.
- **VARA on a Flex (Flex Direct) or an Icom over the network** rides two free virtual audio cables, and the setup finds and pairs them for you. On a radio with a USB sound card no cable is needed.
- **POTACAT levels the audio both ways.** The radio audio VARA hears is trimmed when it would reach the red, which kept VARA waiting for a clear channel. The modem's transmit audio is raised to full scale, so the set power is the power that goes out. The Mail window shows both levels during a session.
- **Start VARA for me when a session needs it.** VARA starts once and stays open between sessions; POTACAT never closes it.
- **Stop means stop.** A session you stopped can no longer come back to life when VARA finally answers, and a new session waits for the last one to hang up.
- **Busy / Clear** on the Mail window's waterfall no longer blinks, and it names the modem you picked, with its own bandwidth.
- **Phone and browser:** a VARA session started from ECHOCAT shows "Starting VARA HF on the shack PC" while VARA comes up, and a failure says what is wrong on the shack PC.
- **ARDOP on macOS** and **packet through Dire Wolf** use POTACAT's own audio link to the radio, so no sound card is picked by hand. A Dire Wolf that fails to start now says why.
- **Create or check your Winlink account** from the Mail window and ECHOCAT Web, and the password rule says what Winlink accepts before you send it.

## Worked All States and worked countries

- **More > Worked All States** shows every US state and Canadian province by band and mode, worked in blue and confirmed in green.
- **On every map,** F6 tints worked states, F7 worked countries, F8 changes the band and mode they are about. It follows your radio by default. The box on the map has the switches and the counts; the **Worked** button beside Map in the FT8 window hides it.
- **Update from LoTW** in the map box downloads your confirmations, and each one now fills in the state and grid the other station certified. **QRZ** beside it fills the state on older contacts from the park or from QRZ.
- **FT8 decodes** show a state badge for a station in a state you still need, and **Hunt: Wanted states** answers only those.
- ECHOCAT Web shows the same tints on its maps.

## Rig profiles

- **A profile for each mode, each band if you like.** Settings > Profiles (also the Rig menu's Presets tab): a profile can set TX power, RF and AF gain, the RX filter, noise blanker, noise reduction, auto-notch, compressor, VOX, preamp, attenuator and AGC. Tick only what it should set; the rest of the radio is left alone.
- **Applied for you:** one profile per mode (all SSB on one, all digital on another), and band rules on top when you want finer control (20 m SSB this, 40 m SSB that). Starting FT8 applies the digital one.

## Radios

- **Ten-Tec Jupiter and Argonaut V** connect through Hamlib with their serial interface powered, the way ACLog's "Power Option = Both" does it. Thanks NO4D.
- **Kenwood TS-2000** on a direct serial connection now gets replies: it needs RTS/CTS handshake, like the TS-590. Thanks WX4MWA.
- **A Hamlib model that is a bridge to another program** (FLRig, TCI, NET rigctl) is no longer offered for a radio on a COM port, and one already set is corrected to your radio's own model.
- **Radio unplugged or switched off:** POTACAT says the port is not there and connects by itself when it comes back, instead of "link restored, link lost" over and over.
- **"None" in the CAT menu or My Rigs stays None,** even with one radio configured.
- **New in the radio picker:** Yaesu FTM-510D (keyed through its data jack, with a serial PTT line for radios with no CAT) and Icom IC-7410.
- **No-CAT radios keyed by the sound card** get a PTT button on ECHOCAT Web.

## ECHOCAT and Guest Pass

- **A guest can listen anywhere.** A Guest Pass no longer refuses a spot outside the guest's licence: a Technician can click a 20 m phone spot and listen. Transmit is checked instead, and a refused key-down tells the guest why.
- **A lighter connection.** The phone no longer downloads your voice macros, settings and worked lists twice on every reconnect, and the power and SWR meters stop flooding it while you receive.
- **Sign in with your callsign.** Settings > Cloud takes "Email or callsign", so an account made with Sign in with Apple and Hide My Email can sign in on the desktop. Thanks KC3SRV, K5AWJ, W9NG and N2PPI.
- Bug reports from a Flex name Flex Direct as the radio's connection.

## Fixes

- **Propagation map:** the arcs to your QTH draw as soon as the view opens, and the dots stay readable in light mode under the night shade.
- **FT8 map pop-out** follows light and dark mode and your colour theme, and has its own Worked button.
- Downloads from the internet never ask for Brotli compression while a fix for a security flaw in it is pending.
