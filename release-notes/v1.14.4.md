**Headline:** Winlink is steadier and easier to diagnose: a dropped link now says what happened and tries once more by itself, the ARDOP link holds on through longer fades, and Report a Bug carries the modem's own side of the story. The Elecraft K4 and Kenwood TS-890S get band scopes, the FT-710 band scope works on a Mac, and POTACAT and ECHOCAT now share the same six themes.

## Winlink

- **Where Winlink works today.** Internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac. ARDOP and packet over the radio run on Windows and Linux. ARDOP on a Mac has had a fix to its audio in this release, but it has not yet worked on the air. No packet transfer has yet been confirmed on the air on any system. VARA HF and VARA FM are your own copy of VARA: on Windows, or now on another computer or under Wine, which we have not tested.
- **A dropped link says what happened.** "Failed connect: HTTP 500" is gone. The status bar and a card now say whether the gateway did not answer, the link dropped after it connected, or you stopped it, what got through so far, how many messages are still in your Outbox, and what to try next. ECHOCAT Web and the ECHOCAT app show the same sentence. Thanks AE5JE.
- **One automatic retry after a dropped link.** When a link to a gateway connects and then drops before the mail is through, POTACAT waits 20 seconds and calls the same gateway once more, on the same frequency and mode. It calls only when nothing is transmitting, the SWR guard is clear and the channel has been clear for 10 seconds; if the channel stays busy for another minute it gives up and says so. It never retries after Abort, after a gateway that did not answer, or after a retry. The countdown, with Try now and Cancel, shows in POTACAT's Winlink Mail window; ECHOCAT Web and the ECHOCAT app do not show it yet. On by default: Settings > Station > Winlink, "Try again once by itself when a link drops".
- **The ARDOP link holds on longer.** A new setting, ARDOP link timeout in Settings > Station > Winlink, is how long the modem keeps trying when nothing is getting through before it ends the link. The default is now 120 seconds (it was a fixed 90), and you can set up to 180 for a path that fades in and out. The gateway has a time limit of its own and may still hang up first, and whether the longer time saves sessions on the air is still to be seen. To make this possible, POTACAT now ships its own build of the Winlink program (Pat) with this one change. Pat is MIT-licensed; the change and its licence are noted in the NOTICE file that comes with POTACAT.
- **Connect works after Abort.** After Abort during a transfer, every Connect was refused with "Pat is still on the last call" until POTACAT was restarted. POTACAT now restarts the Winlink program itself when it is stuck, and checks once by itself 10 seconds after an Abort, so the next Connect goes ahead.
- **Report a Bug tells the modem's side.** Report a Bug now carries ARDOP's own logs from the last three sessions, Dire Wolf's last lines and the frames it heard for packet, and a one-line summary of each of the last 8 radio sessions: gateway, frequency, how long it ran, what moved, and which side ended the link. The summaries survive a restart of POTACAT. The log gets a plain line for each frame the modem sends and hears. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Record what the modem hears.** "Record what the modem hears on the next radio session" in Settings > Station > Winlink saves 30 seconds of what the modem hears and 30 seconds of what it sends, for ARDOP and packet, to attach to a report. Off by default, and it switches itself off after that session.
- **Packet mode on the radio, per rig.** In Settings > Radio, Edit on your rig, "Packet mode on the radio" chooses Automatic, FM (voice) or FM data for packet and VARA FM. FM data is for a radio that takes its rear or USB audio only in its data or packet FM mode (the PKT mode of an FT-857D, for example). Automatic is unchanged: FM data on an Icom, FM on every other radio. Where POTACAT has no command for a radio's FM data mode, it says so and leaves the mode to you. The choice shows once Winlink is turned on. Thanks N5XHI and NA4DQ.
- **VARA on another computer, or under Wine.** VARA HF and VARA FM were Windows only. Settings > Station > Winlink > VARA (your own copy) now has a Host box: leave it blank for VARA on this computer, or type another computer's name or address, then press Test to see whether VARA answers. On a Mac or Linux, run your own VARA under Wine or on a Windows computer on your network; POTACAT never starts VARA there. VARA on another computer uses that computer's sound card. Thanks to jacobsee on GitHub for asking.
- **Mercury is no longer offered for Winlink.** No Winlink gateway runs Mercury, and the mode listed VARA gateways that could never answer. The Mercury HF Data chat window under More is unchanged.
- **No call to a gateway the radio cannot transmit on.** A gateway outside the amateur bands, on a band your rig is not set up for, or beyond the radio's range is refused before anything is tuned or keyed, and the last line of the gateway list says how many were left out. ARDOP and packet now wait for the modem's audio before they tune or key.
- **Steadier modem audio.** The modem's audio now runs on its own, away from the rest of POTACAT. On test Macs the old way lost up to 1.9 seconds of transmit audio every two minutes; the new way lost none on Mac, Windows or Linux. Transmit starts about a quarter of a second later through a sound card.
- **Favourite gateways and History.** The star beside the gateway list saves the gateway you picked; favourites keep their place in the propagation order, marked with a star, and one missing from this list shows at the end. History lists every gateway you have called, with tries, good sessions and the last result, and a click calls it again. The radio's dial sits in the top bar, with Retune when it is off the gateway. A call to a saved or History gateway uses the bandwidth that gateway offers (2000 Hz on ARDOP), not 500. Thanks NJ2DX.
- **Templates.** The Templates button holds Position report, POTA spot and your own templates, with + Add new template, and Save as template while you write.
- **An address book.** To and Cc suggest your saved contacts, groups, and people from your mail as you type. The Address book has contacts and groups, and a message from someone not saved has Add to address book.
- **Position report and POTA spot are forms.** Position report fills the latitude and longitude from your grid and says it goes to Winlink's position map, not a person's inbox. POTA spot checks each park, shows the frequency and mode, and shows exactly what POTA will receive. Spot by Winlink on an activation opens the same form. The To, Cc, Subject and message boxes now look like boxes.
- **A POTA spot by Winlink names your frequency.** From the mail window it gave the gateway's frequency; it now gives where your radio was before Winlink moved it.
- **The busy-channel prompt holds still.** It flickered between two texts twice a second. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Forward works again** on a received message.

## ECHOCAT and ECHOCAT Web

- **ECHOCAT Web's Mail tab, made for a phone.** One screen at a time on a phone, the list and the message side by side on a wider screen, in every theme. The inbox has folders with counts; Send / Receive has the ways to send, band buttons and gateway cards with a GOOD, FAIR or POOR tag; a card says how a session ended, with Try again. A mode that is not offered says why, a failed account check or gateway list has Try again, and with Winlink off at the shack the tab says where to turn it on. Favourites, History, templates and the address book are not in ECHOCAT Web yet.
- **ECHOCAT Web's Mail tab puts the radio back.** Picking a gateway tuned the shack's radio there, and it stayed. It now goes back when you leave Mail or close the page.
- **The FT8 tab shows what the shack is really doing.** After the shack had been idle, ECHOCAT Web's FT8 tab could show old decodes and a moving bar that never updated. It now follows the real state, and opening it while the shack's idle receive (WSPR, PSK31 or JTTY) is running switches to FT8. Thanks KC8ZP.
- **A PC driving a remote shack turns a phone away at once.** A phone paired to such a PC was sent the whole station before being refused, and redialled every second. It is now refused straight away, and a shack whose Cloud link is down is reported as offline instead of "Unexpected server response: 530". Thanks NA4DQ.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **Every park of a two-fer.** When an activator in two or more parks names the others in their own spot ("2-fer: US-1111 US-2222"), JTCAT logs the contact at each park, and the QSO logged card lists them. It reads only the activator's own latest spot, fresh from pota.app, and only when it says 2-fer, 3-fer and so on with exactly that many parks and nothing else; anything unclear is logged at the one park. On by default: "Log every park of a two-fer" in JTCAT's Options. ECHOCAT Web says which parks were added; the ECHOCAT app does not yet. Thanks to ituri (DA2PK) on GitHub for the idea and the first version.
- **Re-spot the activator after a hunted contact.** A new JTCAT option posts a re-spot at the park after you log an activator, with your re-spot template from Settings > Spots. It is public, under your callsign, and skipped if they were spotted there on this band in the last 10 minutes. Off by default. Thanks to ituri (DA2PK) on GitHub.
- **QSO logged is a card.** A logged contact shows a card in the middle of the JTCAT window with the call, band, mode, reports and grid, Edit in log and OK. It never takes the focus from the decode list. ECHOCAT Web and the ECHOCAT app still show their own message.
- **Faster contacts shows the report it sends.** With Faster contacts on, the first reply step still read your grid while the report went out. Thanks to ituri (DA2PK) on GitHub for the fix.

## Band scope and radios

- **Elecraft K4 panadapter.** A K4 connected as K4 Remote (network) now shows its panadapter in the Band Scope, with click to tune. POTACAT changes nothing on the radio. Not yet tried by a K4 owner; a log from one is welcome.
- **Kenwood TS-890S band scope.** A TS-890S on its own USB or COM port at 115200 baud now shows its band scope. POTACAT puts the radio's settings back when the scope closes, and below 115200 baud it says which Baud Rate menu item to change. Not through Hamlib, and not yet tried by an owner.
- **FT-710 band scope on a Mac.** POTACAT looked for FTDI's library under the wrong name on a Mac. Find the file for me in Station Setup now takes FTDI's real file and copies the second file it needs beside it, and the scope card says why if it still will not load.
- **FTX-1 hears GMRS.** A GMRS channel was refused as outside the FTX-1's range. Receive now follows Yaesu's published range, 30 kHz to 174 MHz and 400 to 470 MHz.
- **KX3 with the 2 m module tunes 144.2 MHz.** 2 m is now among the KX2 and KX3 bands (untick it if you have no module). A frequency on a band your rig is not set up for now points to "Check all bands you can use with this radio" in Settings > Radio.
- **CW text on a Hamlib radio that cannot send Morse.** On a Xiegu X5105 through Hamlib, CW text sent nothing while POTACAT showed transmit. It now keys your CW Key Port if one is open, or says what to set up, and never keys the radio without a way to send the CW.
- **FTX-1 CW text plays.** CW typed over CAT was stored in the radio's keyer memory but never played. It plays now, and the Setup instructions cover keying from a CW Key Port.

## VFO and frequencies

- **Frequencies read the VFO way.** Spot tables, maps, Directory, logs, pop-outs, Winlink and ECHOCAT Web write frequencies as the VFO does, 14.074.00 or 7.100.50 MHz, never with a comma. Directory > SWL Broadcasts stays in kHz, as schedules quote it.
- **Band buttons and typed frequencies pick the sideband.** In voice USB or LSB, a band button or a typed frequency now sends LSB below 10 MHz and USB from 10 MHz up and on 60 m, as spot clicks always have. This is in the VFO window, the main window's band buttons and ECHOCAT Web; the ECHOCAT app does not do it yet. CW, AM, FM and data modes are left alone, and the dial and mouse wheel never change the mode.
- **The Small VFO on two lines.** Small VFO settings have Lines: One line or Two lines. Two lines wraps the same items onto a second row, so the strip is narrower.
- **Small and Large keep their own places.** The Large VFO came back wherever the Small one had been. Each size now keeps its own place, saved shortly after you move it, so a crash or an update keeps it too.
- **Power On and Power Off in the VFO window.** The block never appeared. It shows now, the frequency shows dashes while the radio is not answering, and a status line says when the radio has come on or gone off. Thanks to ituri (DA2PK) on GitHub for the fix.
- **A WebSDR that will not come on says why.** Under the SDR row the VFO window says the receiver did not answer, is full, or needs a password, with Try again, and the list marks receivers that failed.

## SSTV

- **The Drive slider keeps 0 and shows its value.** A Drive of 0 came back as 50 percent and was sent at 50 percent. It now stays at 0, shows its percentage, and wraps to a second row in a narrow window instead of pushing the ATU and settings buttons off the edge.
- **No slow-folder waits left in the gallery.** Three more gallery actions no longer wait on a slow Pictures folder. This is our best guess at a freeze one user saw when opening SSTV with no radio; please tell us if it still happens.

## Logging and POTA

- **Tab after the RST jump.** A Tab pressed straight after RST Sent jumped to RST Rcvd moved one field too far, so the received report landed in State. That one Tab is now absorbed.
- **An activation keeps its grid.** A grid you type for a park is kept for that park when the activation restarts, and contacts logged from the phone or FT8 there carry it.
- **The QRZ state fill names the contacts left.** It says which contacts (call, date, band) still have no state and why, and no longer counts Australian contacts it can never fill.

## Themes and Settings

- **Six themes, the same as ECHOCAT.** POTACAT Light, She's On Frequency, POTACAT Dark, Midnight, Acid Burn and Miami Nights, in every window and in ECHOCAT Web. Appearance is Light, Dark or System, which follows your computer. Choose themes in Settings > Display; Appearance also sits in the Settings sidebar, and the Welcome screen has Pick your look. The classic looks and Halloween are gone: your theme moves to the nearest of the six once, and POTACAT tells you which.
- **Settings text is readable in every theme.** Text that was too faint against its background, such as Set up VARA HF for me in She's On Frequency, now passes.
- **The WebSDR directory links open.** The KiwiSDR and WebSDR.org links in Settings > Station > WebSDR did nothing when clicked.
- **The pi stays off the buttons.** The small pi at the foot of Settings could cover Cancel.

## Privacy

- **Report a Bug masks addresses.** The top of the report showed your radio's network address and a remote shack's name or address in full. They are now shortened the same way as in the log lines.
- **No tokens or Guest Pass codes in the log.** The pota.app Connect step, pairing and Guest Pass lines no longer write any part of a sign-in token or a Guest Pass code.
- **No raw memory in Report a Bug.** ARDOP's own logs can hold lines of raw memory. They are now left out of the report.

## Linux and Mac

- **Tailscale on a Mac.** POTACAT could miss a signed-in Tailscale that answered slowly or lived in your own Applications folder. It now asks again with more time, looks there too, and says what it found.
- **The Small VFO clears the Mac window buttons.** On a Mac the frequency sat behind the red, yellow and green buttons.
- **Start at login with a Linux distribution's package.** A package that runs POTACAT on the system's own Electron started an empty window at login. Thanks to mdeltito on GitHub.
- **ARDOP builds with newer compilers.** Building POTACAT's ARDOP modem on Linux failed with GCC 14 and newer. Thanks to mdeltito on GitHub.
