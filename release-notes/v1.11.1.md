**Headline:** FT8 no longer sends the same step twice when your computer decodes a little slowly, SSTV replies start from either the template or the picture, and Kenwood split and meters keep up with the radio.

## FT8

- **The next step goes out on time:** on a slower PC the decode of the slot that just ended could arrive a fraction of a second after the next transmission had already started, so the old message (your grid, or R-19) went out again and the QSO fell out of step. During a QSO, POTACAT now waits up to 2.5 seconds for that decode and then sends the right next step, starting a little late with the end of the transmission still on time. Thanks AB1EX.
- **No more one-second key-up:** when the no-reply limit ended a QSO, it could briefly key the transmitter on its way out. It no longer does.
- **Run is easy to see:** the ULTRACAT Run button was green text on grey and hard to read, especially on the light theme. It now has a dark face when idle or paused and stays solid green while running.

## SSTV

- **Reply in either order:** click a reply template, then the picture you're answering, or double-click the picture first as before. Starting a reply tunes the radio to where the picture was heard, on its sideband, and puts the cursor on their call (or on the report if their call is already known).
- **Their call, wherever you typed it:** if you typed their call over {CALL} in a text layer, it moves into the reply's call box so it fills every {CALL} and the log.
- **Clear the receive picture:** a Clear button and right-click item empty it. A weak picture used to stay there until the next decode replaced it.
- **SSTV and FT8 take turns:** opening SSTV now closes the FT8 and JS8 windows, the same way opening FT8 already closed SSTV. They can't share the radio's audio input. Thanks K3SBP.
- **40 m auto-SSTV is LSB:** the night band was 7.171 USB; 40 m SSTV is LSB.
- **Large template photos shrink:** photos saved in templates before 1.11.0 are resized at start-up, so the templates sync to your other computers again.

## Kenwood (TS-480 and family)

- **Split and VFO follow the radio at once:** changing VFO or split from the radio or a custom CAT button used to take a couple of seconds to show, and could flip the display back to VFO A. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **S-meter while transmitting:** the S-meter no longer moves on transmit or sticks until a restart. Keyed shows power, receiving shows the S-meter.
