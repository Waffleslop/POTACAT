A pot of JTCAT bugs, most of them the kind that leave the radio silent with nothing in the log saying why. Plus a new **Setup instructions** button for whoever is setting up a radio for the first time.

## 🍲 JTCAT fell asleep on slash calls

**N2FSM** left JTCAT hunting overnight and came back to a popout that had stopped transmitting hours earlier. Both times it was stuck on the same station, **WB8YJF/NA67**, and he guessed right: it was the slash.

When one call in a QSO is nonstandard, FT8 sends it as a short code, written `<WB8YJF/NA67>`. An 11-character call plus its two brackets didn't fit in the encoder's buffer, so every report message to that station failed to encode and the radio never keyed. Worse, the QSO never gave up. The try limit counts transmissions, and a message that can't be built never transmits, so the QSO sat there all night and Hunt couldn't move on.

- **Every message to an 11-character call now encodes.** Tested against WB8YJF/NA67 on all five QSO messages.
- **A message that can't be encoded now ends the QSO** instead of freezing it. The log says `Cannot transmit … — QSO abandoned`, Hunt moves to the next station, and Auto CQ goes back to calling CQ.
- **When the try limit gives up on a station, the log now says so.** It used to jump to the next call with no reason given.

## ⏲️ Hunt and the 30-minute limit — please read

**If you have not unlocked ULTRACAT, Hunt now switches itself off after 30 minutes with no QSO progress and nothing from you.** Any completed or advancing QSO resets the timer, and so does choosing a Hunt mode again. A QSO already in progress still finishes, and the popout says why Hunt stopped.

Hunt answers CQs on its own, and that is automatic operation, the same as Auto CQ, which has always had this limit. **With ULTRACAT unlocked, Hunt runs for as long as you leave it on.** ULTRACAT is you taking full responsibility for the station.

## 🔀 Why "Switch FTx" didn't switch

Mode hop only moves to FT4 or FT2 after the idle time **with nobody workable calling CQ**, and never in the middle of a QSO. On a busy band like 40 m FT8 at night there is nearly always someone new to work, so it correctly stays put. The overnight stall above also counted as a QSO in progress, which kept mode hop from ever getting a turn. That one is fixed.

## 🔈 "Switching to 17m…" forever

**AF0DB** (FT-991 on Linux) changed bands and JTCAT never started decoding again. A cycle with less than half a slot of audio is skipped, but the check that restarts a failing audio capture only counted less than a quarter as a problem. So audio arriving at a quarter to half the normal rate gave no decodes, no restart and no log line. Both checks now use the same 50% cutoff. Three short cycles restart the capture, and two in a row are logged with how much audio arrived.

## 📡 FlexRadio: tx/stop/tx/stop at 19:1

My own 8600M, on a 20 m antenna showing 19:1, keyed and unkeyed several times a second. With **SWR auto-tune** on, the SWR guard stopped TX, started the tuner, and re-enabled transmit the moment the tune command was *sent*, before the tuner had tried anything. The tune failed, and the next Hunt answer keyed 69 W into the same fault.

- **Only a match the radio reports as good re-enables TX now.** A failed or silent tune keeps TX off and says so: *ATU could not match … check the antenna, feedline and antenna switch.*
- **Auto-tune is refused above 10:1,** where no tuner will match. It is also limited to once a minute, and it won't retry a band where the tuner already failed this session.
- Switching from WSPR to FT8 no longer restarts a healthy decoder by mistake at the first FT8 cycle, and a decoder restart can no longer start a spare copy that keeps running unused.

## 🌐 Icom IC-7760 over the network

**K3FZT** and **W3AOK** both fought an IC-7760 on the LAN.

- **Test Connection no longer crashes POTACAT.**
- **DATA MOD is set to LAN automatically.** Until now that step failed on every connect and everyone set it by hand.
- **The radio's own CI-V address is used.** A non-7300 Icom with no model selected used to connect perfectly and then ignore every command. POTACAT now uses the address the radio reports about itself.
- **The IC-7760 is in the model list** (200 W, ATU).

## 🛠️ New: Setup instructions for your radio

An FTDX10 owner had no CW until they set **MENU → CW SETTING → PC KEYING** to match the line POTACAT keys, and nothing in POTACAT said so. The rig editor now has a **Setup instructions** button under Radio Model. It shows the radio menu settings other operators needed for that model and connection.

- **The note follows your POTACAT settings.** The FTDX10 note says PC KEYING **DTR** or **RTS** to match what POTACAT will actually drive, and it changes when you change the CW keying line.
- **It's hard to miss when it matters.** The button turns red while a required setting isn't ticked "Done on my radio", and opens by itself the first time you add a rig that needs one.
- **The first notes:** FTDX10 and FT-891 PC KEYING, IC-7300 / 705 / 7610 USB Keying (CW), the Icom CI-V address, choosing your exact model, CAT rate, the Linux `dialout` group, the macOS `cu.` port, and picking your radio's named sound device on Windows.
- **Bug reports now list these notes** and which ones you ticked.

**If your radio needed a setting we don't list, tell us** on Discord or at **k3sbp@potacat.com**, and we'll add it so the next operator with that radio doesn't have to work it out again.

## 🔧 Also in this release

- **Opening POTACAT a second time no longer disturbs the copy that's running.** The second copy was meant to hand over and quit. Instead it tried to open your radio's port, crashed on the ECHOCAT port, and wiped the running copy's logs, so the bug report you filed next had no history in it. It now quits at once.
- **A spot on a half-kHz frequency** (14320.5) no longer loses its highlight the moment the radio gets there. The next arrow key used to start again from the top of the list (**N7HHI**).
