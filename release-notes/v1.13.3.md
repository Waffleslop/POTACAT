**Headline:** Each radio now shows only the bands it can use, everywhere in POTACAT, and you can tick extra bands for a transverter or a 4 m export model. FT8, FT4 and FT2 can be locked to the 1st or 2nd slot. GridTracker accepts the QSOs POTACAT sends it, and contacts logged with the frequency in the wrong unit are repaired.

## Your radio's bands

- **Only the bands your radio has.** Spots, band buttons, the VFO window, FT8, SSTV, JS8, Winlink's gateway list and ECHOCAT Web now offer only the bands of the active radio. A radio POTACAT knows uses its own band list; an unknown model shows every band.
- **Add a band by hand.** Settings > My Rigs, edit a radio: **Check all bands you can use with this radio.** Tick 4 m on a European IC-7300 or FT-710, or 2 m behind a transverter, and that band comes back everywhere. Once you have ticked bands, your list wins over the model's.
- **Please check this after updating** if you use a band your radio's standard model does not list. Your logbook and award views are not filtered.
- **New bands:** 2200 m, 630 m, 13 cm, 9 cm, 6 cm, 3 cm and 1.25 cm.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **TX slot lock.** The FT8 window's **Slot** menu: **Auto**, **1st (even)** or **2nd (odd)**. Locked, every CQ and reply stays in that slot, and a station transmitting in your slot is not called. Clicking one offers **Unlock and call**. Hound mode ignores the lock. ECHOCAT Web has the same **Slot** button.
- **Auto now keeps your CQ in the slot you last transmitted in** for 30 minutes, instead of picking one each time.
- **An FT8 contact with no frequency from the radio** (CAT not connected) is logged on the FT8 dial for the band, so Wavelog and other logbooks no longer refuse it as 0 kHz. Thanks KJ5NVM.
- **WSPR map legend:** the JTCAT window's WSPR map explains its colours: green for -10 dB or better, amber for -11 to -20 dB, red below -20 dB, grey for no report.

## Logging

- **GridTracker sees your QSOs.** GridTracker ignored everything POTACAT sent until it had heard a status message, which POTACAT never sent. It does now, so logged contacts show up. Thanks KC8ZPI.
- **Frequencies in MHz, kHz or Hz.** A frequency typed in the wrong unit is read correctly when it is logged. One that falls in no amateur band is logged blank rather than wrong.
- **Older contacts repaired.** At the first start, POTACAT fixes contacts saved with MHz typed into the kHz box (for example 14.074 kHz for 14074). A backup of the log is kept beside it before anything changes, and the repaired records sync to POTACAT Cloud.

## Rig profiles

- **Monitor on/off and Monitor level** can be part of a profile. Thanks K8IKO.

## Winlink

- **Spot yourself on POTA by Winlink.** For when there is no internet: the Mail window's **POTA spot** button, or **Spot by Winlink** in the Activation panel, writes the spot email to the POTA spot gateway with your park, frequency and mode. An n-fer sends one message per park. Thanks W7RTA.
- **A second VARA session in a row works.** It used to report VARA as not answering until VARA was restarted. Thanks W7RTA.
- **The gateway list error** "invalid character ':' after object key:value pair" is fixed: a damaged gateway list is downloaded again, and only one list is fetched at a time.
- Every hint now says to set VARA's PTT to VOX, matching what **Set up VARA for me** writes.

## Radios

- **A COM port Windows lists is never called missing.** 1.13.2 could hold some radios off with "COMn is not there" when the port was fine (virtual ports, port sharers, some older USB serial drivers).
- **Yaesu voice macros and phone audio** stay in USB or LSB when you transmit voice in SSB over DATA, instead of going out through the narrow DATA filter.

## ECHOCAT

- **An FM radio with its squelch closed,** or a radio with no CAT control, no longer has its audio restarted every minute and a half because the silence looked like a dead link. Thanks KS9B.
- **HF Nets and SWL** are there for a device that connects right after ECHOCAT restarts, instead of a blank Directory tab.
