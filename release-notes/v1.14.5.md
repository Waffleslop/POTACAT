**Headline:** Winlink Mail now has Archive, a Deleted folder you can restore from, and Reply all, and every sent message says how it went out. Packet closes its link to the gateway cleanly instead of leaving the gateway holding it, Winlink gets its own TX level, and a Mac now says plainly when macOS is keeping POTACAT from hearing the radio. Also: Flex LSB slices hear the right sideband again, the WSPR beacon reports the power it really runs at, and SSTV from your phone no longer closes POTACAT.

## Winlink

- **Where Winlink works today.** Internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac. ARDOP and packet over the radio run on Windows and Linux. No packet transfer has yet been confirmed on the air.
- **Archive.** A message in the Inbox or Sent has an Archive button. In the Archive folder, a button moves it back to where it came from. Thanks NJ2DX.
- **A Deleted folder you can restore from.** Delete now moves a message to a new Deleted folder instead of erasing it, so it no longer asks first. In Deleted, Restore puts it back in the folder it came from, and Delete for good removes it after you confirm on the button itself. Deleted empties itself after 7, 30 or 90 days, or Never (30 by default): choose at the top of the Deleted folder, or with "Empty the Deleted mail folder after" in Settings > Station > Winlink. To save the attachments of a deleted message, restore it first.
- **Reply all.** A Reply all button sits next to Reply when a message went to anyone besides you and the sender. The sender goes in To, and everyone else in Cc, without your own address and without anyone twice. Reply stays the plain reply.
- **Each sent message says how it went out.** A message in Sent, Archive or Deleted shows one line under its header, for example "Sent 13 Nov 2026 at 14:32 UTC via W5ABC on 7.102 MHz (ARDOP)." or "Sent 13 Nov 2026 at 14:32 UTC over the internet." Only messages sent from now on have it; nothing is guessed for older mail.
- **Packet closes its link cleanly.** When a packet session ended, by Stop, a stall or a failure, POTACAT shut down the modem before the gateway could answer the disconnect, so the gateway kept the old link open and turned away every call for up to 15 minutes, POTACAT's own automatic retry included. Now the modem stays up until the gateway answers the disconnect, 10 seconds at most, and the status bar shows "Closing the link to" the gateway meanwhile. Press Stop a second time to end it at once. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **A gateway still holding the last link is not called again.** When a gateway answers a call but sends no greeting, the status now says "The gateway is still holding the last link, or its greeting did not decode here. Wait a few minutes and try again. If it keeps happening, check the receive level." POTACAT does not retry such a call by itself.
- **The packet summary blames the right thing.** When the link connected but the gateway's greeting never decoded here, the session summary said the gateway acknowledged none of our data frames, though none had been sent. It now says the greeting never decoded and points to the receive level and the path. Stop also no longer fills the log with refused tune lines. Thanks N5XHI.
- **Winlink has its own TX level.** The Winlink window has a TX level slider next to Power. It sets how loud the modem's audio goes into the radio's sound card for ARDOP and packet (modulation, not RF power); until now that was JTCAT's Drive slider. It starts at the level Winlink already used, then the two are separate. On Flex Direct and Icom Network it is greyed out, because the radio's power sets the level there.
- **The session summary shows the audio levels.** It gives what the modem heard from the radio and what reached the radio at your TX level, and when the audio reached full scale on a sound card it says to turn TX level down to about 50% and watch the radio's ALC. The gap meter no longer counts the normal pause between packet overs as a dropout.
- **Fewer first-run snags.** A grid square is needed to put the gateway list in a useful order, and the list now says so and points to QTH Grid Square in Settings > Station. While gateways are being ranked, Connect says "Ranking gateways... This can take a minute or two." instead of "Pick a gateway first". The reason a mode is greyed out shows for every mode, not only for Internet. Check my account says whether Winlink accepted your password. Messages that said to restart Winlink, which no button does, now say to close POTACAT and open it again. In ECHOCAT Web, a gateway list that never arrives gives up with Try again instead of loading for ever, and with Winlink off the Mail tab names the box to tick.
- **ECHOCAT Web has the same mail actions.** Archive, Deleted, Restore, Reply all, a Cc field and the sent line are in ECHOCAT Web's Mail tab. The ECHOCAT app does not have them yet; an older app's Delete moves the message to Deleted, where it can be restored.

## Mac microphone access

- **POTACAT says when macOS will not let it hear the radio.** When macOS refuses POTACAT the microphone, every audio input is pure silence, and nothing said why. POTACAT now asks macOS once, and when access is refused, or a capture has heard nothing but digital silence since it started, it says: open System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone, turn on POTACAT, then restart POTACAT. The message shows in the main window, in Station Setup's Hearing the radio step, and in the JTCAT and SSTV windows, and it covers Winlink's modem audio too. Report a Bug now includes the microphone permission. A capture that has heard sound once is never judged, and an FM radio with its squelch closed is left alone.

## Radios and band scope

- **A Flex LSB slice hears the lower sideband.** In recent versions a Flex slice in LSB was given a filter above the carrier, so 40 m voice was unreadable while USB and digital modes were fine. LSB and DIGL now filter below the carrier, and AM and FM filters are centred. Not yet tried on a Flex.
- **The FT8 view on a Flex lines up with the pan.** The narrow FT8 view of a Flex panadapter assumed the radio put the pan exactly where POTACAT asked. When it did not, the view showed empty space on one side and cut off the other. The view now follows where the pan really sits, and widens it a little when needed.
- **The band scope keeps running for every device.** With two devices on the shack (the phone and ECHOCAT Web, or a second POTACAT), the scope stopped on all of them as soon as one left its scope view or disconnected. It now keeps running until the last one leaves.
- **Power on the radio's real scale over network Kenwood CAT.** A low-power SDR behind deskHPSDR or Zeus on the Kenwood TCP port got the slider's watts as percent of drive, so 6 W on TX Pwr was 6% drive, and the last position of a slide could be lost. TX Pwr now follows the rig's Full power (W), the last level you set is always sent, and the level is sent again after a band change to a radio that cannot report its power. Not yet tried on a radio. Thanks W5LEE.
- **The FTX-1 shows Power Off only.** Yaesu's FTX-1 cannot be switched on over CAT, so Power On did nothing. It is now hidden for the FTX-1 in the VFO window, the Rig popover and ECHOCAT Web, and Power Off stays.
- **Data-mode audio settings for six radios.** For the IC-7300, IC-7300 MK II, IC-7100, IC-7610, FT-857 and FT-817/818 on USB audio, the setup notes and Station Setup now name the radio's menu setting that lets FT8, Winlink and SSTV audio reach the transmitter (DATA MOD on the Icoms, DIG MODE on the Yaesus), each taken from the maker's manual.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **Replies to a hashed call go out.** When POTACAT's built-in FT8 module had not loaded and it fell back to its backup encoder, a reply to a call shown in angle brackets, such as `<HS0GWL/M>`, failed with "TX ENCODE FAILED" and nothing was sent. FT8, FT4 and FT2 now send it. The log also says at start when the backup is in use, and why. Thanks VK4KTW.
- **The WSPR beacon no longer sends frames back to back.** At 50% TX it could transmit three frames in a row. After a frame the next slot now listens, and the beacon still transmits the share of slots you set. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **The WSPR beacon keeps your power.** If you change TX Pwr while the beacon is armed, that becomes the beacon's power for this radio, kept across frames and re-arms, and your power from before the beacon still comes back when you stop it. After each frame the log gives the radio's own wattmeter reading and says to raise TX Pwr if it stayed at 0 W. A radio on Other Rig (Hamlib) or rigctld Network now shows its real power and moves the PWR meter.
- **The WSPR beacon reports the power it runs at.** The WSPR row's Reported menu replaces the old power box. Auto, the default, reports the power the beacon really runs at, so a 5 W beacon shows on WSPRnet as 37 dBm, not 1 W. You can still pick a fixed value, now from 0 dBm (1 mW) to 60 dBm (1 kW). ECHOCAT Web shows the same menu.

## SSTV

- **Sending a picture from your phone no longer closes POTACAT.** A picture sent from ECHOCAT or ECHOCAT Web could close POTACAT on the shack computer, and a refused picture put up a dialog at the shack that waited for a click. A picture that cannot be sent is now one status line, in ECHOCAT Web and the SSTV window, and the radio is unkeyed first. The ECHOCAT app shows that line once it is updated. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **The radio is on the SSTV frequency before the picture keys.** The picture could start before the radio had moved, on the last Winlink frequency. The radio now tunes first: to the frequency picked in ECHOCAT Web, or else the SSTV channel of the band it is on, and never to a band your rig is not set up for.
- **Closing Winlink Mail leaves the radio where it is.** Closing the mail window took the radio back to where it was before Winlink, even in the middle of an SSTV picture. It now goes back only if nothing has moved the radio since Winlink left it.
- **The frequency box takes what you type.** On 2 m or 70 cm the radio rewrote the box while you typed in it, so 437.550 would not stick. The box now takes kHz or MHz (14230 and 14.230 are the same), and 2 m and up tunes in FM.

## Logging

- **Import Log adds confirmations to QSOs you already have.** Settings > Logging > Import Log skipped a record that matched a QSO in the log, so importing LoTW's confirmations file after your log added no confirmations. It now adds the record's QSL and LoTW confirmations to the matching QSO.
- **Download confirmations can fetch everything again.** After its first run, Download confirmations fetched only newer confirmations, so pressing it before your full log was imported lost the older ones for good. It no longer moves past a confirmation that matched nothing in your log, and Settings > Logging has a new "Download all confirmations again" button that fetches your whole LoTW history once.
- **The DXCC Tracker says worked.** On an ADIF log the tracker counts every entity in the log, confirmed or not, but called it confirmed. It now says worked, for example "Challenge: N worked" and "100 entities worked". A Log4OM log, which counts confirmed contacts only, still says confirmed.
- **KG4 calls in the United States.** A KG4 call with a one- or three-letter suffix, such as KG4OLE, was shown as Guantanamo Bay. Only KG4 with a two-letter suffix is Guantanamo Bay; the others are now United States in spots, JTCAT and the log.

## More fixes

- **Switching operator keeps each profile's own settings.** After you switched operator in Settings > Summary, a save in the second or two before the restart could write the old operator's callsign, logbook and Cloud sign-in into the new operator's profile, and their contacts then synced to the wrong Cloud account. Nothing is saved in that moment now, so a setting changed just before the restart is not kept.

- **Startup says why it did not connect to the radio.** When POTACAT did not connect to the radio at start, it said nothing, and a click on a spot only pointed to My Rigs. It now says why. When WSJT-X Mode is on, the log and the tune message say that POTACAT is leaving the radio to WSJT-X and where to untick it, and Test Connection no longer takes the radio from WSJT-X for the rest of the session. A virtual COM port that Windows does not list, such as one from Win4Yaesu, is now opened anyway when it answers.
- **A net reminder names its net.** In Settings > Spots > Net Reminders, the on/off check box was stretched across the whole row, so a reminder showed only a check mark. Each row now shows the net's name, frequency, mode, start time and schedule beside its check box.
