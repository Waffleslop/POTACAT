**Headline:** Radios with no computer control at all — the Pebble HF and its VOX-keyed cousins — are now first-class in POTACAT, the FT-991A gets every CAT command its manual actually lists, and JTTY had its first QSOs on the air.

## Radios

- **No CAT control (VOX):** a new connection type for radios POTACAT cannot talk to — you tune by hand and the radio's VOX keys on POTACAT's audio. POTACAT connects to nothing, keys nothing and never says "Radio not connected": the status pill stays neutral, a spot click tells you the frequency to dial, and the Station Setup checklist can still prove the radio transmits with a 3-second tune tone you confirm by eye. Also offered on the welcome screen. Thanks N4FFF.
- **Pebble HF** is in the rig picker (Ham Radio Duo). Pick it and the connection type, name and its setup note (VOX on, USB sound card in and out, level set so the VOX just trips, 20 m by hand) follow. A spot on another band says "Pebble HF is 20m only".
- **FT-991A, by the manual:** Artemis went through the FT-991A's CAT reference against what POTACAT sends, and every item was right. The antenna tuner tunes with AC001;/AC002; (POTACAT was sending the Kenwood form); the ATU On/Off toggle works; split is FT3;/FT2; and reads back; filter width uses the 991A's own SH chart; the VFO A | B selector is gone because the radio has no such command (A/B swap stays). The command table in the rig editor now lists Split Off and ATU On/Off too. Thanks Artemis.
- **FTDX101D/MP** tunes the same way (its manual is word for word the 991A's); **FT-891** filter widths use the SH0 form its manual defines. Both from the manuals — an owner's confirmation is welcome.

## JTTY (alpha)

- **It works on the air.** N4FFF made a good run of JTTY contacts with POTACAT on an IC-705, including with other POTACAT users. Thank you, Mike. A WSJT-X 3.2 cross-check is still welcome.
- **Macros you can see and edit:** every macro shows its name with its hotkey beside it (CQ F1, Reply F2, TU CQ F3, My call F4, Their call F5, TU Now F6, Agn? F7, Exch F8). Right-click to rename, change the text or move the hotkey (F1–F12); + adds more, Reset restores WSJT-X's eight. The field is "Their call" everywhere.
- **The right dials:** the band buttons now go to the WSJT team's published JTTY frequencies — 14.090 on 20 m, 7.090 on 40 m. The first alpha put 20 m on 14.080, which is FT4's.
- **ECHOCAT Web:** the Field Day exchange fills under the Field Day profile; a macro edit, profile change or serial bump on the desktop shows on the web at once (and a serial bump on the web reaches the desktop); switching JTTY → FT8 → JTTY no longer leaves the previous session's rows mixed into the list.

## Fixes

- **Transmit with no radio link:** when POTACAT had no live CAT link, the FT8/JTTY "wrong band" check compared against the frequency the *previous* radio last reported and could block every transmission with nothing visible to say so. The check now only runs when a radio is actually connected, and a radio's frequency is forgotten when you switch away from it. Thanks N4FFF.
- The rig editor's setup-notes summary no longer blames "CAT or CW" for a note about audio.
