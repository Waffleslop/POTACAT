Route 66 On The Air started this morning and runs through **Sunday, September 20**. This release exists so you can work it properly — and it fixes a handful of things that were hiding a lot of unanswered FT8 CQs.

## 🛣️ Route 66 On The Air

Twenty-two special-event stations sit at cities along the old road from Chicago to Santa Monica — `W6A` through `W6T`, plus `W6W` and `W6Z`, four of them rovers. Work **any one** of them on any band or mode and you can apply for the certificate; there is no clean sweep and no log to submit. It is the centennial year.

POTACAT treats it the way it treats 13 Colonies: the banner offers **Track It**, tracked stations are badged `R66` in the spot list and on FT8 decodes, and the checklist board ticks itself as you log them. What's new is that these stations have a **place**:

- **They plot where they are.** A DX cluster spot for `W6K` used to land in California, because that's what the callsign says. It's in Oklahoma City, and now the map puts it there — with the city name in the spot's location — and a QRZ lookup never overrides it with somebody's home address.
- **The route is on the map.** Press **Show on map** on the board and the road is drawn from Chicago to the pier, with a pin per station: hollow if you still need it, filled once you've worked it. Click a pin for the city and what you worked it on.
- **Rovers have their own heading** on the board, and every fixed station's row shows distance and bearing from your QTH.
- **The links you actually need** — event site, rules, the certificate application, the station list and the frequency guidelines — sit at the top of the board.
- **Hunt: Event stations** in the FT8 popout answers a CQ from any tracked-event station you still need, and skips the ones you've already got.

### The bug hiding underneath

I opened Contests › Route 66 on opening day and could read about the event but not track it. The drawer ran off the right edge of the window, the one control that starts tracking was under it, and the banner — where most operators discover an event — had been showing America250 all year. America250 runs fifty-two weeks; the banner took the first live event in the feed, and that was always going to be America250. Neither 13 Colonies nor Route 66 ever got the banner.

Now the **shortest live event** gets the banner, **Track this event** is the first button in the Contests drawer *and* on the board of any event you haven't tracked yet, and a one-off contest whose window has passed says **ended** and files itself under Past instead of sitting under "This week" as "starting soon" two months later, which is where WRTC 2026 had been living.

## 🌐 ECHOCAT from anywhere, in a browser

Until now, ECHOCAT away from your own network meant the mobile app. A supporter who wanted their shack from a laptop in a hotel room got a 503.

With POTACAT Cloud, open `https://<yourcall>.potacat.com` from any browser, sign in at **login.potacat.com**, and you get the full ECHOCAT interface — audio, PTT, FT8, logging, the lot. No token to paste. The browser appears in **My devices** like any other paired device and can be revoked from there. Guest Pass holders get an **Open in your browser** button on their pass page.

Settings › ECHOCAT shows the new **Anywhere (POTACAT Cloud)** address alongside your LAN and Tailscale ones, and the tray menu gained **Open in browser**.

Alongside it:

- **Revoking a Guest Pass now reaches the shack.** The button promised the guest would be kicked from the rig immediately; the server agreed, and the desktop never heard about it, so the guest stayed connected until the pass expired. The desktop now ends the session the moment you revoke, and notices within a minute if the revoke came from your mobile device or another computer.
- **Add or change your account callsign from Settings › Cloud.** Accounts created in the mobile app, or with Google or Apple sign-in, had no callsign — and no callsign means no `<yourcall>.potacat.com`. Adding one starts the 30-day trial if that callsign has never had one, and a callsign can be changed once every 30 days. Signing in as a different account also properly retires the old one now; the previous account's sync timer used to keep running underneath.
- The **forgot-password** message for accounts that only ever signed in with Apple or Google (**KC3SRV**) now says what the link actually does: it sets a first password.

## 📻 FlexRadio: four weeks of unanswered CQs at one watt

The WSPR beacon caps your RF power at 1 W while it runs, which is correct. It never gave the power back, which was by design — "raise it in your radio for other modes" — and on a Flex that decision **persists inside the radio** across restarts. Flex Direct shows no SmartSDR power slider, the popout's old "TX Pwr" slider was audio gain that the direct route ignores, and the wattmeter (next item) read 30 dBm as "30 W". My own 8600M sat at 1 W for about four weeks of FT8 with nothing on screen pointing at power.

POTACAT now remembers the power it found before the cap and restores it when the beacon stops, when you change modes, and on quit — and if a crash prevented the restore, the next session's first power reading repairs it. If you deliberately set 1 W yourself, it stays. The popout's **TX Pwr** is now the radio's actual RF power in watts (it turns red at 1 W outside WSPR), **Drive** is the audio slider, and **PWR** is measured forward power. Every FT8 key-down logs `RF power set to N W`, so this can never hide again.

### The wattmeter was reading dBm

Since the Flex wattmeter shipped, it has shown the TX bridge's forward-power meter — which is in **dBm** — labelled as watts. 100 W out read as "50 W". Corrected, and every Flex key-down now logs `[TX] RF out: peak N W, SWR x` so a bug report shows what the radio measured.

## 🔇 IC-7300 on FT8: "I thought no one liked me"

**NA7C** ran FT8 through POTACAT for three weeks with a red TX LED, no power out and no SWR, while WSJT-X on the same radio worked first try. Two bugs, both silent:

- **TX power was three unsynced copies** — desktop, popout and each ECHOCAT client kept their own, and the web client pushed *its* saved value on every connect. One mobile device set to 5% keyed the shack radio with no audio while the desktop slider read 100%. TX power now lives on the desktop, every surface follows it, and the log records the level with the origin of every change.
- **A saved output device that can't be opened no longer keys the radio.** When Windows re-enumerated his USB CODEC, POTACAT logged a one-line warning and played the FT8 tones out of the PC speakers with PTT already asserted. It now refuses to transmit, drops PTT, and says so on screen — in the popout, the main window and on connected clients. Re-plug the device and it heals itself.

If FT8 keys your radio but the wattmeter shows nothing, re-pick **both** Input and Output in Settings › My Rigs › Audio.

## ⏱️ The FT8 clock monitor had never run

POTACAT has carried an NTP clock monitor since June. It was hooked to an event the engine never fires, so it ran only when you pressed Recheck — and in 1.10.15, Recheck threw and showed "Sync: ? (no NTP)". It runs automatically now. More than a second of drift means zero decodes; a few hundred milliseconds means decodes look fine and nobody answers you. If you have been in the second camp, this is worth a look.
