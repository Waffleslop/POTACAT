**Headline:** The VFO window comes in two sizes, Large and Small, and you choose what each one shows. ARDOP and packet Winlink connect far more reliably, you can star the stations and channels you use and tune them from your phone through an SDR, the 40 CB channels are in Directory, and SSTV text gets typefaces, outlines and shadows.

## VFO window

- **A Large VFO and a Small VFO.** The button beside the gear in the VFO window's title bar shrinks it to the Small VFO: one line with the frequency, mode, band, S-meter, Scan, Audio, Volume, PTT and Log. Press it again for the Large VFO. The window opens at the size, place and width you left it.
- **Choose what each size shows.** VFO settings (the gear) has a Large VFO tab and a Small VFO tab. Each is one list: tick what you want shown and move it up or down, or drag it. The Small VFO tab shows a preview of the line. On the Small VFO, the gear opens the Large VFO with its settings, and Done takes you back.
- **The Small VFO stays on one line.** In a narrow window the items at the end of the list hide first. The frequency and PTT always stay. PTT is wide, and Audio sits beside it.
- **Pick your CAT buttons.** Under CAT buttons in VFO settings, choose which of the radio's controls to show: NB, NR, ANF, APF, Preamp, Att, COMP, VOX, MON, RIT, Split, ATU. Only the ones your radio supports are offered. The Small VFO can show your custom CAT buttons too.
- **Scan from the Small VFO.** Scan starts and stops the main window's spot scan, and reads Stop while a scan is running, even one started from your phone.
- **Audio on and Audio off, in words.** The speaker button says whether you can hear the radio.
- **The WebSDR sits under the knob** in a new Large VFO. If you have already arranged yours, your order is kept.
- **A rig preset fits its box.** In a narrow window a preset's settings ran past the edge of its row. They now end with "..." and the full text shows on hover. Thanks hoogskev.

## Directory

- **Favorites.** Star a Landmark (a NOAA weather station, say) or a Channel, and it appears under Favorites in Directory, in the VFO window, and as a marker on the band scope. Favorites belong to each operator profile, and ECHOCAT Web shows them too.
- **The 40 CB channels.** Directory > Channels has a CB (Citizens Band) group with every channel by number, channel 9 marked for emergencies and channel 19 for highway traffic.
- **Tune from your phone through the SDR.** When POTACAT cannot move the radio (no CAT link, or the frequency is out of the radio's range), a tune from ECHOCAT listens through your SDR if one covers it, and connects a listed SDR first if needed.
- **A channel can recall the radio's own memory.** Give a channel a memory number and a tap recalls that memory on the radio, offset and tone included, over Icom CI-V or rigctld.
- **An added net shows Remove.** In Directory > HF Nets, a net you had added showed only a check mark, because the column was too narrow for its Remove button.

## Winlink

- **ARDOP frames keep their leader.** Most ARDOP frames went out with the start cut off, so gateways could not hear the connect request. POTACAT now sends that first audio at key-down. Thanks deadeye007 and NA4DQ.
- **A busy channel can be waited out, stopped, or called on.** Keep waiting now stays chosen, the prompt shows how long it has waited, Stop ends the wait cleanly, and a session waiting for a clear channel is no longer ended as stalled. Thanks deadeye007.
- **Packet on an Icom sets FM data once per session.** On an IC-7100 through rigctld the radio was switched to FM data before every over and keyed late, cutting off the start of each frame. It is now set once, before the first frame. Thanks deadeye007.
- **The gateway list loads, and keeps to your bands.** The list waited on a ranking service that does not exist yet and could take a long time to load; it now loads at once, nearest first. Gateways outside the bands you set for your radio are left out, and the list says how many. Thanks stroopeffect.
- **VARA FM on VOX starts.** A VARA FM set to VOX was refused when its settings still named POTACAT's radio port from an earlier choice. Thanks N5XHI.

## SSTV

- **Text styles.** Each text line on an SSTV picture can have its own typeface, an outline that keeps the words readable after transmission, a shadow, and letter spacing. ECHOCAT Web has the same controls.
- **SSTV leaves a VHF/UHF radio where it is.** Opening SSTV, or Idle Mode starting it, tuned a radio set up only for VHF and UHF to 20 m. SSTV is on HF; such a radio is now left alone and the window says why. A radio with only one of 20 m and 40 m gets that band. Thanks deadeye007.

## Events

- **Stop a banner that runs all year.** Click the event's badge on the banner (the "250" of America250 WAS) to choose Show this banner, Track my progress and the watchlist. With the banner off it stays off, tracked or not. Switch it back on with its Banner box in Settings > Logging > Events & Awards, or in the Spots menu under Events & Awards.

## More fixes

- **CW text over FLRig no longer closes POTACAT.** FLRig has no command for sending CW text, and trying closed the app. POTACAT now refuses the send and logs why. Thanks djrev_11688.
- **The Log button keeps its name.** In the hunter table, the Log button read "L" after a visit to the Nets/SW page. Thanks hoogskev.
- **HOLD TO TALK stays on screen.** On a PC driving a remote shack, a long shack and radio name pushed the HOLD TO TALK button off the status bar. The name now shortens with "...", and the full line shows on hover. Thanks deadeye007.
- **A remote shack's spots stop flickering.** On a PC driving a remote shack, CW and SOTA spots and their map markers vanished and came back on every refresh. Thanks electricfalcon.
- **FT-710 band scope after power-on.** Opening the band scope with the radio off left it stuck on starting after the radio came on. Thanks ride1226.
