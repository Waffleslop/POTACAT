**Headline:** Every contact you log now plays a short coin, and your 10th, 25th, 50th, 100th and 250th contact of the day get their own tune and banner; it never plays on the radio's sound card or while you transmit. Winlink Mail can ask Winlink for weather, propagation and gateway lists, and Cloud sync can no longer delete contacts from your Cloud logbook in bulk without asking you. On radios whose power POTACAT cannot set, such as the FT-857, Tune now asks before it sends a carrier at the radio's own power.

## Sounds

- **A coin on every logged contact.** Each contact you log on this computer plays a short coin, from any log form, JTCAT or WSJT-X. Contacts close together get a shorter coin, and an n-fer makes one sound, not one per park. During an activation, a dupe plays two falling notes instead, and a contact that was not saved plays a low buzz. Imports and Cloud sync are silent. Sounds are on by default; the first coin brings a one-time banner, "New: a short sound each time a contact is logged.", with Keep it and Turn it off.
- **Milestones.** At 1, 10, 25, 50, 100 and 250 contacts in a UTC day, a one-line banner shows at the top of the window for about 3 seconds ("First contact today", "10 contacts today" and so on), and from 10 up a short tune plays after the coin. The banner takes no click and no keyboard focus.
- **Settings > Sounds** is a new tab: the Sounds switch, Contact logged sound with a choice of Sound (Coin, Soft ding, Two-tone, Bell, Morse R), Milestone sounds, Milestone banners, Volume and Sound output. The QSO logged chime that was in Settings > Spots has moved here, and a sound you picked there is kept.
- **Hear them first.** Play beside Sound plays the sound you picked, and the Hear row plays the dupe, not-saved and milestone sounds. Picking a sound or letting go of the Volume slider plays it once.
- **Never on the air.** No sound ever plays on the radio's sound card, and none plays while you transmit: a sound that comes due during a transmission waits up to 3 seconds, then is dropped. If the radio's sound card is the only output on the computer, the pane says the sounds are silent and why.
- **Contacts logged from your phone** make no sound at the desktop; the banner still shows.

## Tune at the radio's own power

- **POTACAT asks before a carrier it cannot turn down.** Some radios have no CAT command for transmit power: the FT-817, FT-818, FT-857 and FT-897, the IC-706MKII, radios behind DXLab Commander, and others. On those, the RF-sense Tune for an external tuner used to promise a low-power carrier and then send the radio's full setting. Now the first Tune on such a radio asks "Tune at the radio's own power", with Tune now and Cancel (Cancel sends nothing), once per radio. The tune card and the log say the carrier goes out at the radio's own power, and the log no longer claims the power was lowered. Thanks N5XHI.
- In the rig's External ATU settings, Tune carrier power is greyed for such a radio, and Ask me again brings the question back. A Tune from ECHOCAT on such a radio waits until you have answered once at the desktop. Radios whose power POTACAT does set tune exactly as before.

## Contact details

- **A new window that shows the station behind a call.** Open it with Details on any Logbook row, or + More info beside the QRZ button in Log QSO. It shows name, town, bearing, distance, local time, zones, your past contacts and confirmations with that station, and with a QRZ.com XML subscription the photo and mailing address (Copy mailing address copies it to the clipboard). Log QSO also gets a one-line summary under the call. The window only shows; it never changes your log.
- **Local weather** for the centre of the station's grid square comes from Open-Meteo. No callsign is sent. It is on to begin with; turn it off with "Contact details: show the station's local weather (Open-Meteo)" in Settings > Station.

## APRS

- Heard you: after a radio beacon or a confirmed radio message, POTACAT watches APRS-IS for 15 minutes and shows which iGate or digipeater heard you, in the APRS window and on ECHOCAT. Usually that is one iGate per beacon.
- **Messages over the radio.** The Messages compose box has Send over: Internet or Radio. Radio is offered while your radio is listening, and the line under the box names the band, frequency and call before you press Send. On 30 m every message is one press.
- **A radio timer, 2 m only, off by default.** Radio timer (2 m only) in the Beacon panel repeats your radio beacon every 10, 20 or 30 minutes. On 30 m there is no timer: every beacon is a click. The 2 m radio timer and automatic radio retries are new and off by default; we have not yet tried them on the air.
- **Better 30 m listening.** More decoders cover a radio a little off frequency, the radio must read back within 50 Hz of 10.1476 MHz before POTACAT says it is listening, a radio left on lower sideband is named in the note, and a narrow FlexRadio receive filter is widened for listening and put back when you stop. If Dire Wolf's connection to POTACAT never opens or drops, the window says so instead of showing Listening.
- **"Nothing was sent" after a beacon that went out.** On direct-CAT radios the radio beacon could transmit and then report that the radio never went to transmit. It now hears the radio's own report. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Start stops FT8** the same way opening SSTV does, so an FT8 engine left running from ECHOCAT no longer blocks APRS.
- **Mac:** the APRS window and the JTCAT and JS8 maps have their close buttons back, and APRS settings and the Map and Messages views can be reached again.
- **A desktop driving a remote shack** can start APRS listening on the shack's radio.

## Winlink

- **Winlink catalog.** In Winlink Mail, press Catalog, tick what you want (propagation forecasts, weather, gateway lists, help files) and press Put in Outbox; Send / Receive sends the request, and each answer arrives later as an ordinary message in your Inbox. A short list is built in; Get the full catalog asks Winlink for the whole list, which fills in by itself when it arrives. Over the radio, POTACAT asks before requesting the full catalog (about 70 KB). Your recent and starred picks stay at the top. Nothing is sent and nothing connects without your click.
- **Add your own gateways.** Under the gateway list, Add a gateway takes a call, mode, dial frequency and notes for a gateway the list does not show, such as a club's. Yours appear in their own group at the top, with Edit and Remove. ECHOCAT Web has the same form under My own gateways.
- **A fresh gateway list.** Refresh beside the gateway list now downloads a new list from Winlink, and POTACAT also does so once a day. In Settings > Station > Winlink (email over radio), Other service codes lists the gateways a club or group keeps off the public list; ask your group for its code. Gateways that need a community-built modem are listed after the standard ones, under their own heading.
- **Delete several messages at once.** Tick the box on each message (Shift-click for a range), then Delete selected; one question in the bar confirms it. Thanks N5XHI.
- **A desktop driving a remote shack** now uses the shack's Winlink: its mailbox, gateways and Send / Receive on the shack's radio.
- **VARA:** a VARA that POTACAT started for a session begun from ECHOCAT is closed when that session is over; a VARA you started, or one a session at this computer has used, stays open. The setting in Settings > Station > Winlink (email over radio) says so. The first VARA call after POTACAT starts should no longer end at once with "did not answer" when VARA opens the radio's sound card itself, as under Wine on a Mac. On a Mac, "Running VARA on a Mac?" links to our guide.
- **Packet:** a gateway on an older AX.25 node that answers the first connect with FRMR is no longer hung up on. Thanks KQ4VPW.
- **FlexRadio:** the Winlink channel markers sit on the signal in DIGU, not 1.5 kHz low.
- **Mercury stays experimental and off by default.** Its Listen no longer holds the radio, so it should no longer block FT8 or Station Setup's Transmit Test. The VARA HF gateway list shows Mercury gateways only when "The modem on this port is Mercury (experimental)" is ticked. Its widest bandwidth and its chat window are hidden until it has been used on the air.
- Winlink internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac. ARDOP and packet over the radio have connected to gateways on Windows; on Linux and the Mac they have not yet been confirmed on the air.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **The RX slider reaches the end of its track.** In the FT8 window, the main window and ECHOCAT Web, 100% is now at the right-hand end; before, it sat a third of the way along and the slider snapped back there.
- New installs now keep the FT8/FT4 transmit tone in the middle of the radio's filter (split, "Fake It") by default. If you already use POTACAT nothing changes: turn it on in the FT8 window under Options. Fake It also no longer blocks transmitting on TCI radios or when no radio is connected, and it stays out of the way while the radio is in split.
- **The automatic CQ tone stays inside the radio's data filter.** It is now picked between 500 and 2400 Hz, so a radio such as the IC-7100, whose data mode passes only 300 to 2700 Hz, no longer sends a low, uneven CQ at 230 Hz. Thanks N4RLW.
- **DXpeditions on the air are back,** as a group in the Band and mode picker (the band-and-mode button, or B). Each shows the call, mode and dial, and says when they run Fox/Hound or SuperFox.
- **Chimes, off by default.** Options... > Sounds: "Chime when a station calls me" and "Chime when transmitting stops by itself", each with a few sounds and a Test button.
- **Calling time.** Options... has "Stop calling after 6 minutes with no answer"; set it from 1 to 30 minutes.
- **More room on small screens.** Options... > The picture: "Show the audio waterfall" can be turned off, and Layout (Automatic, Full, Compact) picks a Compact layout by itself on a short screen such as a 1366 x 768 laptop. Stream / Stations is now a dropdown in the filter row.
- **Fixes:** the JTCAT window remembers whether its map was open; the separate map window no longer puts a station at grid RR73 in the Arctic; the RX level slider's 100% is at the end of its track; JTCAT starts on the band it last used without losing its transmit dial; wrong-band and TCI transmit refusals stay in the window until you close them; and the JTCAT and SSTV windows hear Icom network audio instead of the computer's own microphone.

## CW

- **Enter Sends Message, off by default.** CW macros now have two banks, Activator (Run) and Hunter (S&P), in Settings > Station > Macros, with Load ESM layout for the contest-logger key layout (F1 CQ, F2 exchange, F3 TU, F4 my call, F5 their call, F7 park-to-park exchange, F8 AGN). With Enter Sends Message on, Enter in the call field sends the next message and logs, logging before it sends TU; Esc stops sending. A badge beside Log says what Enter sends next. New variables: {PARK} {MYSTATE} {RST} {NR} {MYGRID}; anything else in braces is never keyed.
- **CW sideband per rig.** In the rig editor, CW sideband can be Automatic (as before), Always CW or Always CW-R (Always CW-U or Always CW-L on a Yaesu), for every band.
- **ECHOCAT Web CW:** a paddle press reaches the keyer before anything else on the page, and a routine settings update no longer cuts an over short. Thanks LZ3AW.

## Logging

- **Export ADIF for a time range or the selected rows.** In the Logbook, Export ADIF now asks what to export: everything shown, A time range (UTC) with From and To dates and times, or the rows you selected. The count and the file name say what is in the file.
- **FT4 and FT2 are written as MFSK.** New FT4 and FT2 contacts go into the log, every logger, QRZ, Club Log and POTACAT Cloud as MODE MFSK with SUBMODE FT4 or FT2, as the ADIF standard and WSJT-X do. POTACAT still shows them as FT4 and FT2, and older records are not rewritten. FT2 contacts can now go to LoTW. The POTACAT Cloud logbook widget shows new FT4 and FT2 contacts as MFSK until a Cloud update follows.
- **Delete and edit hit the contact you clicked.** A second delete in one Logbook session could remove a different, usually older, contact, and a deleted contact could come back after reopening. Both are fixed.
- **Log an amplifier's power.** Settings > Logging: "Always log this power, for an amplifier (W)". Blank by default; when set, new contacts are logged at that power instead of the radio's.
- **The Logbook map shows exactly what the Logbook filter shows,** with a line saying how many stations and contacts it plots. Mode SSB now also finds USB and LSB contacts.
- **N3FJP** gets the call you are working in its entry window, so it shows its lookup and dupe check before you log.
- **Contacts logged from the phone** keep the power, rig and antenna they were logged with.
- **An activation that ran out while POTACAT was closed** ends at launch, instead of 33 seconds after you start working.
- **Guest Pass contacts** logged from your own log form while on a pass carry the lending station's state and county.

## Cloud

- **Cloud sync never deletes your Cloud contacts by itself.** When two logs held the same contact under different IDs, most often two operator profiles sharing one account, a sync could delete the Cloud copies; some accounts lost thousands of contacts that way. A sync now never deletes a contact from the Cloud; removing a real duplicate is yours to do with Find Duplicates. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **Mass deletes wait for you.** If more than 25 deletions would reach your Cloud logbook within an hour, or any deletion of a contact that belongs to another operator profile on this computer, POTACAT holds them and asks: Keep in Cloud, or Delete N from Cloud. The contacts in your local log are as you left them either way.
- **Contacts from your other devices reach your logger.** Contacts logged on a phone, a guest device or another computer that arrive through POTACAT Cloud are now sent on to your logging program, QRZ and Club Log. This is on by default and covers only contacts logged from now on; turn it off with "Also send QSOs from my other devices when they arrive from POTACAT Cloud" in Settings > Logging. A logger that was not running is tried again later.
- **Starting a trial is simpler.** A computer that never had a Cloud account opens the Cloud form on Start Free Trial, and the Cloud Tunnel turns on by itself when your account gets Cloud access, with a notice saying how to turn it off. Accounts that already had access are left as they were. Every tunnel button now reads Turn on Cloud Tunnel.
- **A computer removed from your account** is signed out and says so on the Cloud card.
