**Headline:** POTACAT Cloud is now open to other programs: a documented logging API, included with your Cloud subscription, so any logger, contest program or FT8 app can write to and read from the same log your radio, ECHOCAT and Guest Pass use. Also: your on-air callsign (/P, a visiting prefix) is logged the way the ADIF spec means it, the 1.25 m, 33 cm and 23 cm bands are everywhere, a desktop paddle keys every radio, and FT4 reports a real signal-to-noise ratio.

## POTACAT Cloud API

- **Let another program log to your Cloud logbook.** Settings > Cloud > **API keys**: create a key, paste it into the program. A key writes only to your own log, and every QSO it logs shows up in POTACAT and on ECHOCAT at the next sync. Keys can log and read, read only, also run imports and webhooks, be locked to one website, or write to a test sandbox. Reference and examples at [api.potacat.com/developer](https://api.potacat.com/developer); the same keys are managed at login.potacat.com/keys.
- Programs that already speak World Radio League's JSON shape port by changing the address and key, and loggers that have a QRZ Logbook "API key" box can point at POTACAT Cloud instead. Batches of up to 200 contacts, raw ADIF, duplicates reported instead of dropped, SIG and park fields kept exactly as sent.
- **Import a log into Cloud…** (Settings > Cloud > Logbook Sync) brings in an ADIF export from another logger, skipping contacts you already have, and syncs it. That is the whole move from World Radio League: export ADIF there, import it here.
- **Embeddable Widgets** gain an on/off switch for showing them publicly, and the **on-air badge** (potacat.com/badge) is now the first widget on the list.

## Logging

- **On-air callsign for an activation.** The activator bar has an **On-air call** box: put G8LIY/P or GM8LIY there and that activation's QSOs carry it as STATION_CALLSIGN, while OPERATOR stays your own callsign. Blank works as before. Settings > Station also takes an optional **Owner callsign** (OWNER_CALLSIGN). LoTW signs each on-air callsign with its own station location. Thanks G8LIY.
- **New parks stand out.** Load your pota.app Activations export (Settings > Spot Sources > POTA Activations CSV) and the activator park picker marks every park you have activated; the rest show **NEW**. Parks already in your POTACAT log count too.
- **RUMlogNG** is in the logbook list (Mac). Thanks Albert.
- Logged text with an invisible control character no longer stops Cloud sync. One account's sync had been stuck on it for a week.

## Bands

- **1.25 m, 33 cm and 23 cm** in every band list: spot filters, the log form, the VFO window, band buttons, watchlist band tokens, DXCC and heat-map columns, and FT8 dials (222.065 and 1296.174 MHz). A 1.2 GHz QSO used to be logged with no band at all. The DXCC matrix also gains the 4 m column its rows always had. Thanks to the IC-9700 operator who asked.

## CW

- **A paddle on the desktop keys every radio.** A Vail or VBand adapter (Keyboard mode) or a MIDI paddle only ever keyed FlexRadios from the desktop; it now goes through the same keying path as the ECHOCAT paddle — the QMX's keying line, the CW Key Port, WinKeyer.
- The CW button's volume and speed popup opens inside the window instead of past its right edge. Thanks N6UGP.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **FT4 reports a measured signal-to-noise ratio.** It was sending the decoder's internal match score as the report. Thanks K9CYT.
- **Fine control on TX Pwr, Drive and RX gain:** the mouse wheel steps them one at a time (Shift for five), and clicking the number lets you type it. Thanks K4RVC.

## Radios

- **FT-710 with an mAT-30 (or another tuner on the TUNER jack):** the rig editor's new **Keep the tuner on after a tune cycle** sends the radio its Tuner ON command when it switches the tuner off at the end of a tune. Thanks KF0HOG.
- A radio that keeps transmitting after you release PTT while its CAT link is momentarily down now gets the release as soon as the link is back.
- When a serial port is held by another program, the log says so and names the fixes, instead of only "Access denied".
- A rig list whose active rig pointed at nothing (after deleting and re-adding a radio) repairs itself. Thanks SP5GB.
- The Linux serial-permissions step shows your own login name in the command to type.

## ECHOCAT

- Scrolling inside a toolbar menu on ECHOCAT Web no longer closes it.
- The log now says which audio device your microphone is played to, and after each press whether any microphone audio arrived — the two answers a "PTT works but no modulation" report needs.

## Settings > Cloud

- The tunnel's callsign link is readable on every theme, and start-at-login is two plain checkboxes that show what is set.

## Bug reports

- POTACAT keeps your last three sessions' logs instead of one, and a bug report includes the minutes before you pressed Report a Bug.
