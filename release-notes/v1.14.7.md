**Headline:** APRS arrives: a map of stations near you, listening on your own radio on 2 m or 30 m, one-click beacons over the radio or the internet, messages, and an iGate you can turn on. Winlink gets the Winlink Standard Forms through the bundled Pat. JTCAT now logs a contact only when a 73 is sent or received, and potacat:// tune links can no longer make your radio transmit.

## APRS

- **A new APRS window.** Open it from More > APRS. The top shows three things at a glance: whether your radio is listening, whether you are connected to the APRS-IS internet feed, and how many stations your own radio has heard. Each has one switch. A banner across the top says when an internet beacon timer is running.
- **Listen on your own radio.** Opening APRS starts listening by itself: on 2 m FM if your radio has 2 m, otherwise on 30 m (10.1476 MHz, 300 baud HF APRS). It does not start while you are transmitting, in an FT8 contact, on Winlink or on a Guest Pass. Stations your radio heard get a green ring on the map. Listening only receives; nothing goes out. Your radio goes back to where it was when you stop.
- **It pauses when you move the radio.** If you click a spot or tune away, listening pauses and says where your radio is now, instead of claiming to listen on a frequency it left. Turn the switch back on to return to the APRS frequency.
- **Beacon on the radio, one click.** Press Beacon on the radio to send one position beacon on 2 m or 30 m. POTACAT reads the power back from the radio first and shows power and SWR while it sends. On 30 m it narrows the transmit filter for the beacon and puts it back afterwards. It never beacons on the radio by itself: there is no timer for the radio.
- **Beacon on the internet.** Press Beacon on the internet to send your position over APRS-IS, and use the Internet timer (with a ? that explains it) to repeat every 10, 20 or 30 minutes while the window is open. Nothing goes out on your radio. The line under the button shows the time of your last beacon.
- **Near my home by default.** In APRS settings, your beacon position is "Near my home (about 1 mile)" by default when POTACAT knows more than your 4-character grid square: aprs.fi shows you within about a mile, never at your door. You can choose the grid square or an exact position instead, pick a point on the map, or use "From my radio's GPS" on a FlexRadio with GPS. Also there: My APRS SSID, your map symbol and comment, and adding your current activation to the comment.
- **iGate, off by default.** "Report what I hear to APRS-IS (iGate)" in APRS settings sends the packets your radio decodes up to APRS-IS so they show on aprs.fi. Receive only: nothing from the internet goes out on your radio. It pauses during a Guest Pass.
- **Messages and templates.** The Messages view holds APRS conversations over the internet, with retries and acknowledgements. Templates fill in To and Text for you; nothing sends until you press Send.
- **A tidy map.** Show the last 15 minutes to 6 hours, older stations fade, and Clear empties the map. Clicking a station's label works like clicking its pin. The map opens on your home the first time, remembers where you left it, and the house button under the zoom buttons brings you back home.
- **ECHOCAT Web** has an APRS tab: the map through your shack, the listening and iGate switches, and the internet beacon. Messages and the radio beacon are on the desktop only for now.
- **Good to know.** Decoding uses the bundled Dire Wolf. 30 m APRS is quiet and comes and goes with propagation, and stations using Robust Packet are not decoded. Listening on 2 m and messaging have not yet been tried on the air by us, so please tell us how it goes.

## Winlink

- **Winlink forms.** In Winlink Mail, press Forms, then "Get the standard forms" once to download the Winlink Standard Forms (about 3 MB) through Pat. Pick a form, such as Winlink Check-in or ICS213 General Message, fill it in its own window, and it lands in your message; press Put in Outbox to send. A received form message has View form, and Reply with form when the form names a reply form. Forms need the bundled Pat 1.0.1, which comes with this version.
- **Pat 1.0.1.** POTACAT now carries Pat 1.0.1.
- **Packet: no more dropped first connects.** A gateway that polls the new link while sending its greeting no longer gets cut off by POTACAT's 20-second wait. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Your ARDOP bandwidth holds** for the connect request, instead of falling back to the default.
- **VARA:** Pat now waits for VARA to accept again before it dials, and audio sent through a cable counts on the transmit meter.
- **Transmit on one channel.** In Settings > Radio, edit the rig: "Transmit on" can be "Left channel only" for interfaces that key their own VOX from the right channel, such as the Digirig DR-891 on an FT-891. The default, "Both channels", is unchanged. It applies to FT8, Tune and Winlink.
- Winlink internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac; ARDOP and packet over the radio work on Windows and Linux.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **A contact is logged only when a 73 is sent or received.** JTCAT logs when your RR73, RRR or 73 starts going out, or when theirs arrives. A contact that breaks off before any 73 is no longer logged. When that happens the JTCAT window says "Not logged: W1ABC never sent RR73. Press Send 73 to log it." with a Send 73 button; pressing it sends your 73 and logs the contact. In Run, JTCAT drops the contact and calls CQ again. Field Day logging is unchanged. Thanks K2ECE.
- **A spot click restarts FT8 after a voice spot.** If a voice spot had stopped FT8, clicking an FT8 spot on the band and mode JTCAT already shows no longer leaves it "waiting" forever. Thanks VK4KTW.
- **A finished contact clears the spot target,** and the spot watcher never calls a station you already worked.
- **The quiet transmit frequency** is measured even when no JTCAT window is open.
- **Fake It** never mistakes its shifted dial for home.
- **A remote desktop's JTCAT map** centres on the shack's grid square.

## Tune links

- **potacat:// tune links never make your radio transmit.** Links like potacat://tune/14074/FT8 tune POTACAT the same way a spot click does. Before tuning, a link now stops Run, Hunt, the WSPR beacon, a spot target set to call and any contact in progress, and shows a notice saying so. A link that changes band no longer starts the automatic ATU tune; press Tune yourself if you need it.
- **A link that starts POTACAT waits for the radio** to connect before tuning, and says so if the radio does not connect within a minute.

## Logging

- **The frequency is kept to the hertz,** from the spot to the Logbook and every export.
- **Multi-park contacts.** A contact with two or more parks sent to ACLog or another logger names every park in its comment and park fields. QRZ Logbook gets every park in the park list and the first park in its single-park field.
- **Guest Pass contacts carry the station's location.** A contact made on a lent station now records the lending station's grid square, state and county, not the guest's home, and an activation contact takes the park's location. Such contacts go to LoTW in their own batch with that location.
- **Logger32:** the logged-QSO comment is cut at 50 bytes, so accented park names no longer break it.
- **Prior contacts** opens its card again.
- **A home call signed in a park abroad** takes the park's country.
- **The worked-band map** counts a contact with no band on the band of its frequency.

## Radios

- **Yaesu FTDX101D and FTDX101MP** are now separate choices, at 100 W and 200 W.
- **CW Key Port turns break-in on** the same way CW text over CAT does, so DTR keying transmits. Thanks W2XYZ.
- **Power Off shows where POTACAT can turn the radio back on:** by default for direct serial and CI-V rigs, not for Hamlib (rigctld) rigs, with a box in the rig's settings to change it.
- **Zeus through its TS-2000 CAT port** switches to data mode for FT8 and FT4.
- **Icom IC-7300 MK II:** the band scope note no longer asks for a USB baud setting the radio does not have.
- **FlexRadio:** a saved band power setting is no longer read as live power.
- **WSPR** never changes band during its own transmit slot, and a receive-only WSPR band hop runs no ATU tune.
- **Mac:** every serial port closes cleanly at quit.

## ECHOCAT

- **ECHOCAT Web CW:** the sidetone and what goes on the air always agree.
- **Contacts logged from the phone** are confirmed once they are on disk and are never logged twice, and the phone's park is kept across midnight UTC.
- **ECHOCAT Web reconnects** when the connection silently dies or the screen wakes. Its error banner shows the actual message on iPhone and ignores scripts the browser itself adds.
- **ECHOCAT Web keeps the worked map in step** after each contact without reloading the whole list.
- **"QSO Logged" on the phone** appears only once the contact is actually saved.

## Remote and startup

- **Restart from the phone no longer strands you.** A forced restart from ECHOCAT no longer stops the Remote Launcher along with POTACAT, so POTACAT comes back and its helper programs are cleaned up.
- **Faster start.** The first window appears sooner, most noticeably after an update.

## Also fixed

- The Small VFO's frequency can be typed and clicked again.
- The radio audio monitor mutes while you transmit.
- FreeDV shows RADE in the VFO window and ECHOCAT, logs its audio to the session log, and on Mac runs the same microphone check as JTCAT.
- FreeDV spots show one row per station, and departed stations leave.
- SSTV paints the last line and keeps clean dark pictures.
- JS8's wrong-band guard follows the knob onto JS8 frequencies and says why it refused.
- A stalled QRZ lookup gives up at its timeout.
- Settings help and error messages now name the real places: Settings > Radio and the rig editor's own labels.
