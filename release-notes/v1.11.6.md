**Headline:** Log a contact for the other operator in your shack without switching operators, highlight spots by state for Worked All States, and the FT8 courtesy 73 that sometimes never went out now always does. The IC-7610 keeps its DATA mode and filter, there is a Text size control in Settings, and a radio that prints a startup banner no longer fails Test Connection.

## Logging

- **Log as another operator.** Two of you share one PC and take turns on the radio? Set both of you up as operators (Settings › Summary › Add operator; each has their own callsign, logbook, QRZ Logbook key and Cloud account), and the Log QSO dialog, the Log QSO pop-out and ECHOCAT Web show a **Log as** chooser. Pick the other operator and that contact goes into their logbook under their callsign, is uploaded with their QRZ key, forwarded to their logbook program, and synced to their Cloud account the next time they sign in. No restart, no switching. Your own worked-before marks, worked parks, event progress and Logbook window are untouched, because that contact is not yours. The Save button says whose log it is going to ("Save for W9XYZ"), the chooser goes back to you each time the dialog opens, and the confirmation names the operator. A Guest Pass never sees the chooser. FT8 auto-logging and the Activation logger stay with the active operator. The ECHOCAT app will get the same chooser in an upcoming update. Thanks KD9WI.

## Spots

- **States in the watchlist.** A watchlist entry can be a state instead of a callsign: `US-NJ` or just `NJ`, `CA-ON` for a province, with the same optional band and mode as a callsign (`WY:20m`). In the plain watchlist it gives the star and the desktop notification. In one of the three colour groups it outlines every park spot from that state, so a group named "States I need" with its own colour becomes your Worked All States tracker: delete a state from the group as you work it. A park on a state line counts for either state. A friend's callsign or a `W1AW/*` wildcard still wins over a state, so friends keep their own colour. Thanks N0KAH.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **The courtesy 73 always goes out.** After a QSO was logged, POTACAT waited a fixed two decode cycles for the 73 (or the RR73 when you called CQ) and then closed the QSO, whether or not that message had ever been transmitted. If the decode that advanced the QSO landed a moment after the next transmit slot had started, the previous message went out once more and the QSO closed with the 73 never sent, calling CQ and hunting alike, and the only remedy was to double-click the other station's report. The QSO now stays open until the 73 or RR73 has actually been transmitted, bounded so a transmission that can never happen still closes it. Thanks IK4IDF.
- **Clock-sync banner readable on every theme.** The clock warning at the top of the JTCAT window had colours that fought the theme and buttons too small to read. It now uses the same red, amber and green as the rest of the window, with matching buttons.

## Radios

- **IC-7610: DATA mode and filter are kept.** Icom's DATA command takes two bytes (which DATA mode, which filter) and POTACAT sent one. The IC-7300 and IC-7100 tolerated it; the IC-7610 did not: FT8 left USB-D2 as plain USB with no error anywhere, and every spot click reset the filter to FIL1. POTACAT now sends both bytes, re-selects the DATA1, DATA2 or DATA3 the radio itself reports instead of assuming DATA1, and remembers the filter you use in each mode so switching to SSB brings back your SSB filter, not your CW one. The log shows "DATA mode DATA2 FIL2" when it changes. Thanks WF4I (#96, #97).
- **Test Connection after a startup banner.** Some radios print a banner on the serial port before their first CAT reply. POTACAT took the banner for a bad reply and reported a missing frequency response. A complete frequency reply is now recognised even with noise in front of it, and the diagnostic says when bytes arrived but none of them were a CAT response. Thanks KK4UDJ (#88).

## Look and feel

- **Text size in Settings.** Ctrl + and Ctrl − have always resized the whole window, and many never found them. Settings › Display now has **Text size** with − and + buttons, a percent readout and Reset. It applies at once, the keys still work from anywhere, and the readout follows them. Searchable under "font".
- **Dark variant** (Navy / Charcoal) sits on its own tidy row under Dark Mode, without the "(classic)" and "(modern, OLED-friendly)" labels.
