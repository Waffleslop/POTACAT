**Headline:** Eleven fixes from the first week of the public bug tracker, and WSJT-X's "Fake It" split for JTCAT. A Yaesu left keyed after a CW message from your phone is now unkeyed, Check for Updates always says what it found, VARA FM's port settings are left alone, a "Log as" contact goes to that operator's Club Log and Wavelog accounts, and the FT-710 band scope restarts itself when it stalls.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **Split operation "Fake It".** In the FT8 window's Options, tick "Keep my transmit tone in the middle of the radio's filter (split, "Fake It")". For each transmission POTACAT moves the radio's dial in 500 Hz steps so your tone goes out between 1500 and 2000 Hz, then puts the dial back. Your signal lands on the same frequency, and a tone near the edge of the passband is no longer cut by the radio's filter. FT8, FT4 and FT2; the radio needs CAT control.

## CW

- **A Yaesu left keyed after a CW message is unkeyed.** After a CW message from ECHOCAT or the desktop, POTACAT checks up to three times that the radio has dropped out of transmit and unkeys it if it hasn't, also when the CAT link dropped during the message. Before, an FTDX-10 could stay keyed until you touched it.

## Radios

- **Xiegu G90 through rigctld.** Polling the G90 as it dropped out of transmit crashed the bundled rigctld during FT8, and the unkey waited for the restart. POTACAT now leaves the radio alone while it unkeys, and a rigctld restart that owes the radio an unkey is never held back by the restart limit.
- **RTS line: "Asserted, no flow control".** The rig editor's RTS line setting gains this choice for a CAT cable that runs through a box which needs RTS up but does no flow control, such as the PC port of an LDG YT-1200 tuner. When a radio never raises CTS under RTS/CTS flow control, the log now says so.
- **A serial port stuck on CTS can be closed.** Under RTS/CTS flow control with a far end that never raised CTS, the port could not be closed after the link went down, and every reopen said "Access denied" until POTACAT was restarted. The port is now drained for a moment and then closed, and the close survives switching the rig to None.
- **The FT-710 band scope restarts itself.** When the scope helper stops delivering frames, POTACAT restarts it, and when the radio goes away and comes back the scope starts again. Nothing is restarted while you transmit.
- **SCU-LAN10 scope on the FTDX10 (experimental).** The scope line is read from the first part of the frame, as on the USB cable, so the FTDX10's scope over the LAN adapter draws instead of staying blank. Thanks VK4KTW for the real-radio log.
- **VARA FM's port settings are left alone.** 1.14.0 could write COM0 into VARA's PTT port and set VARA FM's PTT option, and VARA then showed a "port COM0 unable to open" dialog that blocked unattended connects from a phone or tablet. POTACAT no longer blanks VARA's port lines or sets that option. Thanks N5XHI.

## Logging

- **A "Log as" contact goes to that operator's accounts.** When the Log QSO window logs a contact as another operator, the Club Log and Wavelog uploads now use that operator's account, not the active operator's. Club Log uploads go through an outbox, so a slow or failed upload no longer holds up the save.
- **Guest Pass contacts no longer wait until a restart.** The queue that sends a guest's contacts could stop after a round with nothing to send, and the contacts waited until POTACAT was restarted.

## Receive

- **WebSDR: a spot click no longer brings seconds of noise.** Retuning a WebSDR reset the audio decoder while the server's encoder carried on, so the audio came out as clipped noise until the next quiet moment. Contributed by ituri.

## Updates

- **Check for Updates always says what it found.** On Windows the Check for Updates link at the bottom of Settings could return to the main screen with no message. It now says that POTACAT is up to date, that an update was found, or what went wrong.
