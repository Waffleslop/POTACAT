**Headline:** SSTV receive works again, FT8 auto-sequencing moves on every cycle on slower PCs, and ECHOCAT's mic comes through strong on USB-codec radios like the IC-7300. Plus a stuck-paddle fix, a (tr)uSDX model, and FTDX10 filter widths that survive a band change.

## SSTV

- **Pictures decode cleanly again:** since 1.10.18, band noise just before a station's leader tone could pull the decoder up to 188 Hz off, giving dark, speckled or tinted images. Since 1.10.24, a station 50-100 Hz off frequency in noise often failed to start at all. The decoder now measures the station from the leader itself and retunes before reading the header. Mistuned stations decode better than in any earlier version.
- **SSTV uses your radio's audio:** the SSTV window listened to the computer's default microphone unless you had picked a device there, while FT8, JS8 and ECHOCAT used the radio input from My Rigs. SSTV now follows My Rigs for both receive and transmit. A device you chose in the SSTV window still wins, and a device that has gone away is refused with a message instead of silently replaced, so a picture never plays out of the PC speakers with the radio keyed.
- **The decoder stays on:** closing the SSTV screen in ECHOCAT stopped the desktop decoder while its window still said "Listening". It keeps running while the desktop window is open, and says "Decoder stopped" if it ever does stop.
- **Idle SSTV stays put:** Auto-RX SSTV no longer cancels itself a second after starting, and picks the day or night band instead of always 20 m.

## FT8

Thanks to George AB1EX.

- **Auto-sequencing moves on:** the more sensitive decoder from 1.10.24 takes longer, and on a slower PC the reply was decoded after your next transmission had already started. Every step of the QSO went out twice. While you are in a QSO the decoder now finishes before the next slot, keeping what it has found so far if time runs short. When you are only listening it takes as long as it needs.
- **Tries limit works again:** the same timing stopped the tries count from counting, so a stalled QSO could repeat forever. Fixed.

## ECHOCAT

- **Stronger mic on USB-codec radios** (KM0JPR): on an IC-7300 or other radio fed through its USB audio, your mobile device's mic went out at plain unity level, TX drive above 100% was ignored, and TX EQ + Compression did nothing. They all work there now. For a louder, punchier signal, turn on Settings > ECHOCAT > TX EQ + Compression.
- **Receive comes back sooner after PTT** (OH7DSQ): on a SignaLink or USB-codec radio, the tail of your speech kept a VOX-keyed interface in transmit after you let go, so the first words of the reply were lost. Your mic now reaches the radio only while you transmit.
- **Web paddle no longer sticks** (LZ3AW): the first paddle press of a session could key the radio until you pressed STOP. Fixed.
- **Signal reports on the web** (KW4FM): tapping RST Sent or Rcvd selects the value so you can type over it, and RST Sent moves to RST Rcvd once a full report is typed, like the desktop.

## Radios

- **(tr)uSDX** (KQ4VUU): a new "(tr)uSDX" radio model. The truSDX driver carries the radio's audio over the same USB link as CAT, so POTACAT's once-a-second poll clicked in your receive audio. This model polls every 30 seconds and never while transmitting. Pick it in My Rigs.
- **FTDX10 and other Hamlib radios keep your filter width** (N3FMC): a band change that flips USB to LSB reset the width to the mode default (2450 Hz on the FTDX10). The width now stays where you set it.
- **TS-480 meters** (LZ3AW): the wattmeter, S-meter and SWR are calibrated from real readings. 100 W no longer reads 70 W, S9 reads S9, and a 2:1 match reads 2:1.
- **Split on Kenwood radios:** the web VFO no longer lags a split change.
