**Headline:** POTACAT now runs a Zeus or Thetis SDR over TCI, with no sound card: tuning, receive audio, the band scope and, on Zeus, FT8 and ECHOCAT voice transmit. FreeDV digital voice receive is fixed on Windows and arrives on Mac and Linux for the first time, and the phone hears the decoded speech. Guest Pass owners get Take the radio, Give it back and Cancel pass, every contact records the rig it was logged on, and a long list of Yaesu, Kenwood, Elecraft and Icom fixes from a check against each maker's CAT manual.

## Zeus and Thetis over TCI

- **A new kind of rig.** In Settings > My Rigs, add a rig and choose "Zeus or Thetis (TCI server)", then pick the Program, Host and TCI port and press Test connection. POTACAT tunes the radio, follows its dial and mode, reads the S-meter, and sets the filter, squelch, AGC, noise blanker, noise reduction and attenuator through the TCI server. If the program is not running yet, POTACAT keeps trying every 5 seconds. The setup note says how to turn on the TCI server in Zeus or Thetis.
- **Receive audio with no sound card.** A new TCI rig takes its receive audio from the server, so JTCAT decodes and draws its waterfall, the VFO window's monitor plays, and ECHOCAT hears the radio without any audio cable.
- **The band scope from the radio itself.** The band scope can come from the server's IQ stream, in the More menu, ECHOCAT Web and the app. If no IQ arrives within 3 seconds it says so.
- **Transmit on Zeus.** JTCAT's FT8 and the other digital modes transmit through Zeus, and so does ECHOCAT voice from your phone's microphone. The PTT button, voice macros, CW, Tune, SSTV and the ATU do not key a TCI rig yet. Power on a TCI rig is a percentage of drive. Thetis stays receive only until a Thetis owner has confirmed transmit. Not yet tried on a radio: please tell us how it goes. Thanks W5LEE, whose work this is built on.

## FreeDV digital voice (receive)

- **RADE works on Windows.** Receiving RADE V1 no longer closes POTACAT at the end of each over, the whole over is turned back into speech, and the speech plays at the right speed. Decoded speech in every FreeDV mode plays back to back instead of overlapping. Thanks W3MDT.
- **New on Mac and Linux.** The Mac (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux builds now decode FreeDV (700D, 700E, 700C, 1600) and RADE V1. This is new, so please tell us how it sounds on the air.
- **Turn it on.** Tick "FreeDV Digital Voice" in Settings, then click a FreeDV spot, or pick RADE in the VFO window's mode list.
- **Your phone hears it.** With FreeDV running, ECHOCAT and ECHOCAT Web hear the decoded speech at about the level of SSB audio, where they heard silence before, and starting FreeDV from ECHOCAT now starts it on the shack.
- **Transmit comes later.** FreeDV transmit is not built yet, so PTT is off in FreeDV modes, from every surface, rather than sending ordinary voice on a FreeDV frequency. FT8, SSTV, Winlink and Tune still key.

## Guest Pass

- **Take the radio, Give it back, Cancel pass.** The Share Your Rig window shows who has the radio and three controls for the owner. Take the radio works Now or In 5 minutes, with an optional short message to the guest, and a pending take can be called off. Give it back hands the radio back, and Cancel pass ends the pass once you confirm with "Yes, cancel the pass". Thanks NA4DQ.
- **The guest may take it back after 5 quiet minutes.** When the owner has held the radio for 5 minutes with no transmitting, keying, tuning or mode change, the guest is told the radio is free and can press Take the radio. Nothing switches by itself; the old automatic 2-minute hand-back is gone.
- **The guest always knows where they stand.** A guest is told in plain words whether they are on the air, waiting and why, reconnecting, or the pass is over, and a short Cloud hiccup no longer ends a pass. With two devices on one pass, "Use it here instead" moves it without dialling again.
- **A guest can use the station's own SDRs,** switching between and tuning the ones the owner set up. Adding a new SDR is refused.
- **Passwords stay with the owner.** A guest no longer receives SDR passwords saved inside an SDR address, and a guest's device no longer receives the owner's full settings. Settings Export no longer carries those passwords either; importing on the same computer restores them.
- **A guest's contacts reach the guest's own logger.** A contact made on a lent station now goes on to the guest's own external logger, such as MacLoggerDX, through the guest's POTACAT.

## Logging

- **Each contact records its rig.** Every new contact stores the name of the rig it was logged on (from My Rigs) in the ADIF MY_RIG field. The Logbook has a Rig column, off by default, that you can edit. Thanks W5LEE.
- **Contacts from ECHOCAT are marked.** A contact logged from ECHOCAT or ECHOCAT Web is marked as such, with an optional Source column in the Logbook. Thanks W5LEE.
- **DXCC numbers in every record.** Logged and forwarded contacts carry the DXCC entity number, so N3FJP and other loggers place the country correctly.
- **The right country for more calls.** Alaska (KL7), American Samoa (KH8) and prefixed portable calls such as 6W/IK2HKT now log the right entity, UA3ABC/9 is Asiatic Russia, and KH6XX/1 stays Hawaii.
- **JS8 and JTTY by name.** JS8 and JTTY contacts show as JS8 and JTTY everywhere, in the Logbook, counts, filters, worked-before and the DXCC tracker, instead of MFSK.
- **Logger32 keeps the date.** Contacts sent to Logger32 no longer arrive dated 30/12/1899. Thanks I3FIY.
- **Choose where TQSL is.** Settings > Logging has a TQSL program field with Browse, and on Linux POTACAT also finds TQSL installed as a Flatpak.
- **QRZ Logbook says whether each upload went.** Each upload writes a line to the log, and a refusal shows "Logged <call>, but QRZ Logbook did not take it:" with QRZ's reason. Checking your QRZ subscription no longer turns the upload off.
- **Cloud sync on a second computer.** When this computer's log holds fewer contacts than your Cloud logbook, Sync Now pulls the whole history again instead of reporting nothing to do. Sync also writes what it pulled, any errors, and why it is not running to the session log.

## Radios

- **Yaesu FTX-1 and FT-710: CW messages end cleanly.** Stop now ends a keyer-memory message at once, and a radio still in transmit after a CW message is released automatically instead of waiting for Stop. Thanks W2XYZ and DA2PK.
- **Yaesu SCU-LAN10: transmit audio over the network.** With the radio's LAN audio as the audio source, voice, voice macros, JTCAT FT8 and FT4, Tune, SSTV and CW text now go over the network, and JTCAT keeps hearing after the first transmission. An option for each rig mutes the radio's speaker and sidetone while POTACAT has the radio. Winlink over LAN audio is not supported yet. Tested on an FT-710 by DA2PK, thank you.
- **Yaesu FT-710, FTDX10 and FTDX101: power meter while transmitting.** The PWR meter shows forward power on the air, on the FT-710 from an owner's own measurements.
- **Yaesu, checked against the CAT manuals.** On the FT-891, FT-991/991A, FTDX10, FTDX101 and FTX-1, filter widths use each radio's own table, AGC Off and Auto are no longer swapped, PROC and the preamp work, RTTY and the data modes read back correctly, power starts at 5 W, and CW text plays through keyer memory 5 on the FT-891 and FT-991A. The FT-891's RF gain covers its whole range, CW break-in delay is on the right scale on the FT-710, FTDX10 and FTDX101, and the FTDX5000's power reads and sets correctly. These came from the manuals rather than from a radio, so owners, please tell us if anything is off. Thanks I3FIY for the FTDX5000.
- **Yaesu FT-991/991A split.** Setting the transmit frequency for split now works.
- **Yaesu FT-891: set DATA BFO to USB.** A new setup note: the FT-891 takes the data mode's sideband from menu 08-12 DATA BFO, which is LSB from the factory, so FT8 and Winlink went out on LSB until it is set to USB.
- **Yaesu radios through rigctld read their power.** POTACAT now reads the power setting of a Yaesu on rigctld, so the Station Setup 5 W test transmit runs instead of stopping.
- **Kenwood TS-480: transmit audio from the right socket.** This fixes the 1.14.5 change that left a TS-480 with an interface on the front microphone jack sending no audio. In Settings > My Rigs, "Transmit audio input" chooses Rear (data / ACC2 socket) or Front (microphone jack) for the TS-480, TS-590S/SG, TS-890S and TS-990S, and a TS-480 asks you on its first transmit.
- **Kenwood TS-590S/SG: the ATU tunes.** The tune button now starts the tuner, including a remote tuner. Thanks VA2PSL.
- **Elecraft K3, KX2 and KX3: the ATU tunes.** The ATU button did nothing; it now starts the tuner. The KX2 and KX3 paddle hint now names Elecraft's own menus. Thanks KH7QO.
- **Icom: the voice hint names the right menu.** When voice transmit shows no power, the hint gives each Icom its own menu path, and the IC-7610 setup note explains unlinking the CI-V USB Port before raising its baud rate for the band scope. Thanks KQ4UUR.
- **An amplifier drive limit for each rig.** In Settings > My Rigs, "An amplifier is connected: limit the drive power" sets a power POTACAT never goes above, from every slider, FT8, WSPR, Tune, a guest or ECHOCAT, and POTACAT turns the radio back down if it is found above it. Off unless you set it. Not yet tried on a radio.
- **No Power Off button that strands you.** Power Off is hidden for radios POTACAT cannot switch back on, which is most radios on a USB cable. A box in the rig's settings allows it anyway.
- **PTT on RTS with RTS/CTS flow control.** The rig editor and Station Setup now catch this combination, which cannot work, and offer two ways out. Station Setup's test transmit counts only when the radio really keyed. Thanks W5PLC.
- **SSB-over-DATA leaves FM and AM alone** on radios without an FM data mode, so a KX3 on 2 m FM no longer jumps to USB data when you transmit. Thanks KS9B.
- **A band-scope SDR on the radio's IF output.** Choose "The radio's IF output (stays on one frequency)" when your SDR is connected to the IF, so the picture holds still as you tune.

## CW

- **The VFO window's PTT no longer sticks on "ON AIR"** after a CW text macro. Thanks DA2PK.
- **The ECHOCAT Web paddle keeps better time.** Fewer extra dots and dashes at speed, and a cut tone fades instead of clicking. Thanks LZ3AW.

## Winlink

- **Where Winlink works today.** Internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac. ARDOP and packet over the radio are built in on all three; on the Mac neither has been confirmed with a gateway yet.
- **See the level going into the modem.** Next to Busy/Clear, the Winlink window shows the modem's input level, for example "Input -18 dBFS". When the radio's audio is too loud it says "Input too high"; turn down the radio's USB or data audio, or the sound device's input, so the modem can hear when the channel is clear.
- **A black waterfall explains itself.** When no radio audio reaches POTACAT, the waterfall says so and why. Thanks N5XHI.
- **Sessions keep the data mode.** A Winlink session now keeps the radio in its data mode even when JTCAT's "Switch radio to DATA mode" box is off.
- **Packet on an FT-817, FT-857 or FT-897 through flrig** puts the radio in PKT, so the gateway hears the call. Thanks N5XHI.
- **On a Flex, the panadapter centres on the Winlink channel** during a session, then goes back to your view.
- **The dial stays on the gateway** while your phone's Mail tab is open after a session started from the phone.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **A Stations view.** Band Activity in the JTCAT window has a Stream and Stations switch. Stations shows one line per station heard, kept up to date and sortable, with Need (a new country, state, grid or call) and Park columns. Stations drop off after 2, 5 or 10 minutes, and a double-click answers.
- **Clicking a second caller mid-QSO** now drops the first QSO instead of logging it and sending a 73 over the new one.

## Spots, ECHOCAT and more

- **One spot age everywhere.** The VFO window, the Spots pop-out, ECHOCAT's spot list and the SmartSDR and TCI panadapters all use the table's Max Spot Age, so an old spot no longer comes back on the VFO card.
- **The band filter follows your rig.** Switching from an HF radio to a VHF-only one no longer empties the Spots table, and the bands you turned off are remembered.
- **A remote PC mutes the shack's audio while it transmits,** ending feedback on radios whose USB audio carries the transmit monitor.
- **Space as the talk key no longer toggles scan.**
- **Screen readers** now name the rig on every control in the rig list.
- **Directory arrow keys** move from the row you clicked.
- **SSTV's Open folder** makes the folder if it is missing.
- **A Launcher start** no longer makes the next start report a crash.
