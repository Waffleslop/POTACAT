**Headline:** An RTL-SDR on your network becomes a second receiver: tune to NOAA weather or a 2 m repeater your HF radio can't reach and POTACAT plays it through the SDR, then hands you back to the radio the moment you tune where it can go. The Directory gains Landmarks (WWV, beacons, NOAA and more, each explained) and Channels, a frequency chart you can edit and import from CHIRP. KiwiSDR listening works again on the receivers' new firmware.

## Your SDR as a second receiver

- **Listen through an RTL-SDR on your network.** An RTL-SDR running rtl_tcp (or an SDRplay running rsp_tcp), on this PC or a Raspberry Pi by your antenna, goes in the same list as WebSDRs: Settings > Station > WebSDR, **Add SDR**, and type its address and port (for example 192.168.1.50:1234). POTACAT recognises it when it connects and demodulates the signal itself: USB, LSB, CW, AM and FM.
- **The SDR takes over where your radio can't go.** Tune a frequency outside your radio's range (NOAA weather on 162.475 MHz, a 2 m repeater on an HF radio) and POTACAT tunes only the SDR, plays it everywhere you listen, phone included, and shows its frequency with an SDR badge. Tune back to a frequency the radio covers and you hear the radio again at once; the SDR stays connected, ready. Transmit is refused while you are listening on the SDR, with a message saying why. If the SDR isn't connected yet, POTACAT connects it for you.
- **One volume knob.** The volume knob in the VFO window now sets the SDR's audio too.
- **It shares the dongle with the band scope.** If the same RTL-SDR is your band scope (Settings > My Rigs > Band scope from an SDR), listening uses that connection.

## Directory

- **Landmarks.** A new tab in the Directory: signals worth dialling into at any time, each with what you'll hear, why it matters and something to try. WWV, WWVH, CHU and RWM; the NCDXF beacons with the one transmitting right now; NOAA weather; the calling frequencies; and a few oddities. A first-time hint suggests WWV on 10 MHz to check your radio, antenna and audio in one go. A landmark only your SDR can hear is shown with an SDR tag. Thanks N0KAH and KC8DHX.
- **Channels.** A frequency chart that is yours to edit: FRS, GMRS and its repeater pairs, MURS, the marine VHF channels with what each is for, aviation calling and guard frequencies, public-safety interop and the EMS Med channels to start with. Drag to reorder, edit, delete, add your own, **Import CHIRP CSV** from your HT's programming file, and **Export CSV** back. Deleted starter channels stay deleted; **Restore starter channels** brings them back. A click tunes, through the SDR when your radio can't reach it. Channels also appear in ECHOCAT Web's Dir tab. Thanks KL4XQ and KC8DHX for the lists.

## Band scope

- **On a Flex, the FT8 window shows the radio's own waterfall.** With Flex Direct, the FT8 window's waterfall is now the radio's panadapter, zoomed to the frequencies the decoder listens to and centred on them: 3 kHz wide for FT8, FT4, FT2, JTTY and PSK31, and as narrow as the radio goes for WSPR's 200 Hz window. The decode window is shaded, your transmit frequency is marked, and a click sets the audio frequency. The old audio waterfall returns while the scope starts, when SmartSDR is running the radio, and in multi-slice.
- **The Band Activity strip under the map is off by default again.** Turn it on under Settings > Display > Band Activity Heatmap. Thanks WD3D.

## Radios

- **KiwiSDR listening works on the new firmware.** Many KiwiSDRs updated to firmware 1.902, which sends no audio unless the listening settings arrive later in the connection than POTACAT sent them; POTACAT gave up after 8 seconds. Fixed. A KiwiSDR whose owner allows only its own web page now says so, instead of going quiet after a short preview, and the receivers that allow no apps have left POTACAT's built-in list. Thanks N9ZX.
- **An Icom in DATA mode keeps its FT8 preset.** On an Icom, starting FT8 applied the FT8 preset, then the radio's own report of "USB" brought the SSB preset straight back. And starting POTACAT no longer applies a preset over the power you set by hand. Thanks K4RVC.
- **A PTT port keys on RTS by default.** A Digirig keys PTT on RTS; POTACAT's default and its advice said DTR, so PTT never keyed. Existing rigs keep the pin they have. Thanks K5LEB.

## Winlink

- **Radio sessions on a radio with no CAT control.** Packet, ARDOP and VARA now run on a "No CAT control" radio, such as an HT with a Digirig: you set the radio to the gateway frequency yourself, and POTACAT keys it by VOX or the PTT line you chose. Thanks NA4DQ.
- Internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac; ARDOP and packet over the radio work on Windows and Linux.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **The WSPR beacon runs as long as you set.** The 30-minute limit is gone. The first time you turn on Beacon TX, the FT8 window asks how long it should run; **Run** beside Beacon TX changes it any time. It runs until you stop it unless you choose a time. You remain responsible for your station while it transmits. Thanks K2BDE.

## ECHOCAT and remote

- **A desktop that loses the rig to your phone stays off it.** When ECHOCAT took the rig from a desktop driving the shack, the desktop reconnected within a second and took it back, over and over. It now stands down and waits for you to press Connect again. Thanks W0EGR.

## Logging and activations

- **N3FJP fills in the DXCC for US contacts.** The record sent to N3FJP names the country "USA", the name N3FJP's DXCC lookup uses. Thanks AE5JE.
- **A two-fer fills both parks.** In the activation logger, clicking a hunted activator at a two-fer or n-fer fills every park for park-to-park credit, not just the first. Thanks WD3D.
- **The activation toolbar wraps** at full screen with a large text size, so Spot, Export ADIF, Logbook and Settings no longer sit off the edge of the window. Thanks WD3D.

## Installing

- **Upgrades no longer stop at "POTACAT cannot be closed".** The installer now asks POTACAT to close, waits for it to shut down properly, and stops anything POTACAT left running, before it replaces the files. Thanks W0TLP and KB2UXB.
