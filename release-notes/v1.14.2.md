**Headline:** Packet and ARDOP Winlink behave on more radios, a refused transmission no longer leaves a tone playing, Custom CAT toggles show the radio's real state, FT8 can decode from a WebSDR, a forgotten activation ends by itself, and each shack can have its own POTACAT Cloud address.

## New

- **A Cloud address per shack.** Each shack can have its own POTACAT Cloud address, up to 3 per membership, so ECHOCAT can switch between shacks over Cloud. Name the shack in Settings > Cloud.
- **FT8 from a WebSDR.** JTCAT has a Receive audio choice: Radio (sound card) or WebSDR. With WebSDR, the SDR follows JTCAT's band and its audio feeds the decoder and waterfall. JTCAT won't transmit while the WebSDR is the receive source, since a WebSDR can't hear your own signal. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **Custom CAT toggles show the radio's real state.** Each toggle can have a Read command and an ON when rule (for example IF; and 33=1 for split on a TS-480). POTACAT reads it at connect, after each press and when the panel opens, never while transmitting. A toggle with nothing to read shows ? instead of claiming Off. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **Switch the shack's radio on and off from a remote POTACAT.** Away from the shack, the CAT chip's popover shows whether the radio is on and has Power On and Power Off buttons. Power Off asks once more, and it is refused while the radio is transmitting. Power On tells you if the shack can't reach the radio's port. Needs a radio POTACAT can already power on and off.
- **Prior contacts.** Right-click a spot in the hunter table or a call in the FT8 decode list and choose Prior contacts to see how many times you have worked them, when you last did, on which bands and modes, and your last five contacts. Hover over the worked check mark for a one-line summary. It reads only your own log, never QRZ.
- **A forgotten activation ends by itself.** If you leave an activation running, it ends at the UTC day change or after 6 hours with no contacts, whichever comes first, and POTACAT tells you. Contacts after that are logged as home contacts. Contacts already logged are never touched.
- **TX power in the VFO window.** The VFO window has a Power row under the filter slider. It follows the main window's power control and uses the same limits, including Full power (W) and a Guest Pass cap.
- **The HOUND chip turns Hound mode on as well as off.** In the JTCAT window the chip is now always shown, dimmed when Hound is off, so one click turns it either way.
- **QRZ upload is easier to find.** The Logging tab now points to QRZ logbook upload, which lives under Station with your QRZ login (it needs a QRZ XML subscription). Go to QRZ upload takes you there.

## Logging

- **A hunter contact with a POTA and SOTA activator uploads the SOTA chase.** When the activator was spotted on both programs, the contact is logged under the park with the summit alongside, and the chase never reached SOTAdata. It does now. If SOTA upload is on but no SOTAdata login is saved, the log says so instead of staying silent. Thanks WV8T.

## Winlink

- **An Icom stays in DATA mode for the whole session.** POTACAT switched the radio from USB to USB-D and back on every ARDOP frame, 20 mode changes per attempt, undoing the DATA mode it had just set. A Winlink session now puts the radio in DATA once and keeps it there until the session ends. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **The radio stays put between attempts.** After an attempt, the radio no longer jumps back to where you were, with two band changes and an ATU tune each time. It goes back once, when you close the mail window or stop Winlink, and the log says where it is going.
- **Packet Winlink keys the radio again on a rigctld rig.** Since 1.14.0, POTACAT waits for the radio to confirm DATA mode before it keys, but for FM over rigctld it never sent the mode command, so packet on 2 m never transmitted and the log said "not keyed" on every over. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Packet Winlink: longer TX delay and tail, now settable.** Dire Wolf's TX delay was fixed at 300 ms, too short for a radio keyed over CAT, so gateways missed the start of each frame. The defaults are now 500 ms and 200 ms, and both are in Settings > Station > Winlink next to Packet speed. The end-of-session log no longer says "Dire Wolf decoded nothing" after Pat has connected. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **A packet session longer than five minutes is no longer cut off.** The connection to Pat was being closed by a five-minute limit underneath, which ended slow 1200-baud sessions with "fetch failed". The log now names what happened if Pat drops the connection. Thanks N5XHI.
- **"This server cannot key the radio" only when it is true.** Through Hamlib, a rejected reply to another command could be blamed on the keying command and lock out transmit for the rest of the connection, with "PTT FAILED" before every key-down while the radio keyed fine. The verdict now needs the real reply, and is withdrawn when a later key-down is accepted.
- **The log shows what Dire Wolf hears and what each over looked like.** Every frame the radio received over packet is logged, with a count at session end, and each over logs when PTT was pressed, when the audio ran and how loud it was.

## Transmit

- **Icom IC-7300 MK II over Icom Network: set DATA MOD to LAN.** A MK II on Icom Network keyed with no power until the radio took its transmit audio from the LAN. Station Setup now says so, with the menu path, and the "keyed but no power" hint names it. Thanks KJ5CHK.

- **A refused transmission plays no audio.** With the LAN as the audio source on a Yaesu SCU-LAN10, Tune played a tone through the PC speakers until POTACAT was closed. Now when POTACAT refuses to key (SCU-LAN10 audio, SWR guard, licence class, tuner calibration, SDR listening), Tune plays nothing, an SSTV picture is not sent, and a voice macro stops at once. Thanks VK4KTW.
- **The VFO window says which microphone it used.** Every PTT press in the VFO window logs the microphone it opened, the output it played to and the level, and shows a notice when the microphone is the radio's own receive audio, is blocked, or is silent. Thanks KD9OUT.

## VFO window and SSTV

- **Mouse wheel up tunes up.** In the VFO window, scrolling up moved the frequency down. It now matches the radio, ECHOCAT and the scope, and a sideways scroll does nothing. If you got used to the old direction, it is flipped. Thanks W0TLP.
- **Opening SSTV can't freeze the app on a slow Pictures folder.** The SSTV gallery folder is now prepared in the background with a 3-second limit. If it doesn't answer, the gallery is skipped and the log says so. This is our best guess at a freeze one user saw with no radio connected; please tell us if it still happens.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **FT8 started from your phone recovers its audio like a desktop start.** Restart audio, the engine's own silent-capture recovery and a device change from the phone did nothing for FT8 started from ECHOCAT, which showed "No decodes" until Restart audio was pressed. They work for a remote start now, and the log says what each restart did and why.
- **JS8: a refused transmission is not "fully transmitted".** When POTACAT refused to key (wrong band, SWR guard and the rest), the log said the message was fully transmitted. It now says it was not, and why, and that the heartbeat was dropped. The "radio has never reported its MODE" note no longer appears before the radio has answered at all, and a port that reopened before the radio answered says so instead of "Radio link restored". Thanks KI7LIK.

## Winlink compose

- **The message hint matches the session.** Composing for VHF packet said "Keep it short over HF." The hint now follows the mode: VHF packet, VARA FM or HF. Thanks NA4DQ.

## ECHOCAT

- **HALT is never hidden.** ECHOCAT Web hid the HALT button in CW and RTTY and whenever the radio's mode had not arrived. A safety stop is always visible.
- **Tailscale on a Mac shows the real state.** A leftover Homebrew copy of the Tailscale command could answer for a daemon that was never signed in, so POTACAT said "installed but not signed in" while the menu-bar app was signed in. POTACAT now asks every copy and uses the signed-in one, and the status says signed in but turned off, awaiting approval, or really not signed in, with what to do. Checking no longer pauses the app.
- **A rare bad certificate.** About 1 time in 512, POTACAT generated an invalid certificate for ECHOCAT's connection, so the phone could not connect until POTACAT was restarted. Fixed.

## Band scope and spots

- **FT-710 scope: a SCU-LAN10 you already had on stays on.** Pressing Enable on the band scope now checks the radio first. If SCU-LAN10 was already on, POTACAT leaves it alone and no longer turns it off when the scope closes. If POTACAT turned it on, it still turns it back off. Thanks to the #113 reporter (mdeltito on GitHub).
- **A spot on a park and a summit shows both.** The spot table shows both program pills and both references, e.g. IT-0843 / I/UM-069. Thanks to ituri on GitHub for the fix.

## More fixes

- **Off-grid activations reach MacLoggerDX.** Contacts from an off-grid ECHOCAT activation arrive later through Cloud sync; they're now sent on to your external loggers (MacLoggerDX, the main logbook, QRZ Logbook, Club Log), once, and only for your own station's phone contacts. Thanks NA4DQ.
- **Two profiles on one Cloud account keep their logs apart.** A profile no longer pulls QSOs that belong to another profile on the same PC, and the duplicate check never removes another profile's contacts. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **The Log QSO form fits.** It opens wider, can be resized from its corner (the width is remembered), and its fields stack instead of squeezing at larger text sizes. Thanks hoogskev.
- **VFO window audio comes back after a radio power cycle.** Powering the radio off and on from the RIG button used to leave the VFO window silent until it was reopened. Thanks ride1226.
- **Map markers stay put.** Spot markers update in place instead of being redrawn on every refresh. Thanks electricfalcon.
- **Icom baud rates.** The Icom setup offers 4800, 9600, 19200, 38400, 57600 and 115200.
- **Band scope spots follow Max Spot Age**, and old spots leave the scope even when no new ones arrive. Thanks VK4KTW.
- **Remote Launcher certificate.** About 1 time in 512 the Launcher made an invalid certificate and kept it for a year. Fixed, and a bad saved one is replaced.
- **Licence filter:** spots marked DATA, DIGU, USB-D, WSPR and similar are no longer wrongly hidden as outside your privileges.
- **ECHOCAT web keyer:** a paddle released just before the next element no longer sends an extra dot or dash. Thanks LZ3AW.
