**Headline:** The FT8 window is rebuilt to be easier to read and use, with one button for band and mode, large action buttons and new Hunt targets. A Guest Pass is now locked down to operating the station, ECHOCAT can be set to Cloud only or Off, and FT8 on 2 m is no longer refused by the license check. DXLab Commander joins the radio types.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **One button for band and mode.** The FT8 window's toolbar has one button, for example "20m FT8", that opens a picker: mode chips over a grid of the bands your radio has.
- **The buttons are the actions.** Call CQ, Hunt, HALT, Tune, ATU, Logbook and Options sit in two rows, with what CQ sends shown beside it. The decode lists, QSO tracker, meters and Options are larger and labelled in words. Double-click a station to answer it, as before.
- **New Hunt targets:** New countries, DX only, Same as Call and Watchlist. Hunt answers what the badges and highlights already show, and still answers only a CQ.
- **Review a contact before it is logged.** Tick "Open the Log QSO window before saving" in the FT8 window's Options, and each finished contact opens in the Log QSO window. Nothing is written until you press Save QSO. Contacts wait their turn, and the heard grid, Field Day exchange and parks are kept. Thanks AE5JE.
- **The comment "JTCAT FT8" is now a setting.** Settings > Logging has a checkbox for it, on by default. Untick it and JTCAT leaves the comment empty.
- **The FT8 window shows when the engine was stopped.** A voice or CW tune, or Stop on your phone, used to leave the window reading 20m FT8 with nothing running. It now says so and restarts the engine.
- **Decoding recovers by itself after the PC sits idle.** If the sound card stops delivering audio, capture is rebuilt and the log says why. Before, ECHOCAT Web's FT8 could stop decoding after the rig sat idle. Thanks KC8ZP.

## Your license

- **FT8 on 2 m is no longer refused.** The license check stopped at 6 m, so 2 m, 1.25 m, 70 cm and higher bands were "outside your privileges" for every class. It now covers those bands, the CW-only bottoms of 6 m and 2 m, all of 60 m, the Technician CW-only segments, and US Advanced. The spot filter uses the same table.

## Guest Pass and ECHOCAT

- **A Guest Pass can operate the station and nothing else.** A guest can tune, transmit, change modes and levels, use the scope and log their own contacts. Anything else is refused with a message on their screen: raw CAT commands, your logbook, your Cloud account, your settings, and Winlink mail.
- **The pass's power limit is applied at the radio.** The radio is set to the pass's power cap when the guest starts, and your power is put back when the pass ends. A radio that does not report its TX power over CAT cannot transmit during a Guest Pass. That includes a Flex used through the CAT ports without the SmartSDR link.
- **Split is checked against the transmit frequency.** With split on, a guest's transmission is judged on the frequency the radio will actually transmit on, not on the dial.
- **ECHOCAT can be Cloud only or Off.** Settings > ECHOCAT > Who can reach this computer:
  - **Local network and Cloud** works as before.
  - **Cloud only** closes ECHOCAT to your local network and Tailscale, while your POTACAT Cloud address and Guest Pass keep working.
  - **Off** closes it completely.
- **Removing a device closes all of its connections.** That includes a second connection when more than one device at a time is allowed.
- **One shack per Cloud address.** Before, two desktops on the same account shared the Cloud address and split your phone's connections between them without saying so. Now a second desktop asks before it takes the address: Use this shack instead, or Keep the other shack. Turning the Cloud Tunnel off on one desktop leaves the address with the other. Thanks KQ5O.
- **SSTV on ECHOCAT Web no longer sticks on Receiving.** Before, a decode that ended without a picture left the Send tab locked.
- **ECHOCAT Web's FT8 list button says Clear**, like the desktop.

## Radios

- **DXLab Commander is a radio type.** POTACAT controls the radio through Commander's TCP port. It handles frequency, mode, split, PTT and CW text, and Commander command sequences cover controls Commander has no command for. Contributed by David S.
- **PTT on the CAT port's RTS or DTR line.** In the rig editor, Transmit (PTT) offers "RTS on the CAT port" and "DTR on the CAT port" for Serial CAT and Icom (CI-V) rigs. This is for radios such as the IC-706MKIIG, which has no transmit command, when an interface on the CAT port keys the radio. The Icom editor also gains "The audio interface keys it".
- **A radio with no CAT control keyed from ECHOCAT now gets your phone's voice.** The radio keyed but the audio was silent. Thanks KS9B.
- **Icom repeater memories keep their offset.** With SSB over DATA on, an ECHOCAT transmission on an Icom in FM used to take the radio out of its memory channel, so the repeater offset was lost. Thanks NJ7N.
- **A 60 m SSB spot tunes USB.**
- **The external tuner (RF-sense) tune puts the mode back, DATA included,** and checks that the radio took it. Before, one lost command could leave an IC-7300 in AM. It also works with ECHOCAT off, and its AM carrier no longer triggers a rig preset.
- **Serial CAT Test Connection opens the port the way the live link does.** A radio set to RTS/CTS flow control no longer fails the test while CAT works. The FT-817/818/857/897 are tested with their own command. Thanks W7OSG.
- **Test Connection names a port another program holds,** with ACLog and World Radio League as examples, instead of saying it does not exist. Thanks NO4D.
- **rigctld problems are named.** The log now says when rigctld keeps dropping because another program holds the port or the baud is wrong. It also says when the Port field holds a Hamlib model number instead of 4532. Thanks WX4MWA.
- **The FT-817/818/857/897 no longer try RTS/CTS flow control.** It could key an interface wired to RTS. Thanks KL4XQ.
- **The selected rig's own port is used.** A connection setting left over from no rig in My Rigs no longer makes POTACAT open the wrong port.
- **CW on Yaesu radios.** The stuck-transmit safeguard now runs only on Yaesu radios, where it is needed, and only when the radio has reported it is transmitting. Stop clears the TX banner at once. An unchanged speed from ECHOCAT no longer sends a speed command before every macro. Thanks WF4I and LA8JKA.
- **Idle Mode waits while a phone is connected**, instead of moving the radio while you watch it from ECHOCAT. WSPR, JS8, JTTY and PSK31 also tune Icom radios in DATA mode now. Thanks W0TLP.

## SDR and band scope

- **A chosen SDR no longer freezes the VFO readout** on the previous spot (GitHub #117).
- **The SDR has a volume slider again** when the VFO window's meters are hidden (GitHub #118).
- **The scope pop-out works like SmartSDR.** Spot labels sit inside the trace, a click tunes to the spot, and you can drag the frequency scale to change the size of the trace and the waterfall. Double-click the scale to reset it.
- **FT-710 band scope setup says what is wrong.** A 32-bit LibFT4222 no longer counts as installed, and a helper that failed to load says which of its faults happened. Thanks W0TLP.

## WSPR and Winlink

- **WSPR spots are reported at the start of their 2-minute window**, not when decoding finished. Thanks W5JSS.
- **A slow PC still decodes WSPR.** When wsprd takes too long, POTACAT switches it to a faster search instead of losing every window. Thanks N5IRU.
- **Winlink over ARDOP no longer stops on a Mac** at the first connect request.
- **The VARA HF gateway list arrives.** A list ranked by propagation now gets two minutes. Thanks I5EKX.
- **Starting Dire Wolf no longer closes POTACAT.**
- Internet mail works on Windows, Linux and Mac; ARDOP and packet over the radio work on Windows and Linux.

## Logging, spots and display

- **JTCAT records carry the QSO's real start and end times**, CQ and ITU zones, continent and, for home contacts, the city. A failed JTCAT log now says so on screen. The Log QSO window gains FT2.
- **The Logbook fills the window** when it is maximized.
- **The selected spot's operator name is used** in CW macros when two spots share a frequency. Thanks N8JMS.
- **CW spots use one connection** to rbn.telegraphy.de, filtered by the clubs you choose on that site. Thanks DJ1YFK.
- **Two new themes:** Miami Nights and She's On Frequency, in Settings > Display > Theme.

## Installing

- **Upgrading closes anything running from the POTACAT folder** before it installs, and the install log records why an upgrade fails. Thanks W0TLP, WV8T and N7FQT.
