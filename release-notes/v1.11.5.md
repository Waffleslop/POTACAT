**Headline:** DXpeditions now live in the Contests view, live and upcoming, and POTACAT finally tells a DXpedition from a contest station. The ATU Tune button works on Icom and Hamlib radios, a TS-590 over Hamlib transmits its audio, and the VFO window only shows the meters your radio actually reports.

## DXpeditions and contest stations

- **DXpeditions in the Contests view.** Live and upcoming DXpeditions sit in the same list as contests, sorted into Live now, This weekend, This week and by month. Each row shows the dates, how long it runs, bands and modes, operators and QSL route. "On air" means someone spotted the station in the last two hours (PSKReporter, the Reverse Beacon Network, the DX cluster or POTA/SOTA/WWFF spots). "Running" means today is inside its announced dates, with the time left. Click a row for the details, a link to the announcement, its on-air page on potacat.com, and **Add to watchlist**. Dates come from NG3K's listings; DX-World announcements that only give dates in prose show as "Dates not announced" until someone hears them.
- **Choose whether to see them.** "DXpeditions" and "Contest stations (DX)" are two new entries in the Contests view's **Sources** menu. Untick either to hide it; your choice is remembered with the other categories.
- **Is your DXpedition flag on?** A bar above the list says whether DXpedition spots are switched on. Waiting for a DXpedition next month with the flag off means it would go by unmarked when it is finally spotted, so when it is off the bar says so and offers **Turn on DXpedition spots**. A row whose own source (Club Log, DX-World, DXNews or NG3K) is switched off in Spots › DX Expeditions is marked "not flagged", and its details say why.
- **CONTEST is not DXP.** The DXpedition feed announces contest operations too, and POTACAT used to label them all DXP. A station on the air for a contest weekend (PJ4K for CQ WW SSB, 3B8M and FS/K0CD for CQ WW CW, the RTTY weekend entries, a contest call named inside another announcement like CQ3W for CT9/DF7EE) now carries a violet **CONTEST** badge in the spot table, the map popup and the map window, with a note such as "Contest station: CQ WW SSB". DXpeditions keep the red DXP badge. Only DXpeditions are pinned to the top of the spot table and get the DXpedition map pin, so a contest weekend no longer buries your spots. A DXpedition that also enters a contest stays a DXpedition and says "also in CQ WW CW".
- **Contest stations sit on their contest weekend.** Most contest announcements carry no dates of their own, so POTACAT takes them from the contest list: PJ4K and CQ3W appear beside CQ WW SSB on 24-25 October, JW5X on the SAC weekend rather than its team's whole week in Svalbard.
- **Home calls are no longer flagged.** The feed listed operators' home calls next to the expedition's call (K0CD next to FS/K0CD, DF7EE next to CT9/DF7EE, and 39 more), so their ordinary spots from home carried a DXP badge. They don't any more. The feed itself now marks these, and POTACAT also catches them on its own.
- **Better DXpedition details:** NG3K's "fm Longyearbyen" (meaning "from") was read as the mode FM and leaked into the operator list; the country now always shows.
- **ECHOCAT:** the phone now receives your DXpedition spot settings from the desktop, and a change made on the phone shows on the desktop at once. The ECHOCAT app update that uses this is on its way.

## Radios

- **ATU Tune works on Icom and Hamlib radios.** On a Hamlib (rigctld) radio, the Tune button only switched the tuner on and never started a tune; on an Icom connected by USB, CI-V over TCP or Icom Network it sent nothing at all. Both now send a real tune command (Hamlib's tune cycle; CI-V 1C 01 02). Yaesu and Kenwood radios are unchanged. Whether an IC-7300 starts an external tuner on its tuner jack from this command, as it does from its own TUNE key, still needs a confirmation from an owner. Thanks N4RDX.
- **TS-590SG / TS-890S / TS-990S over Hamlib now transmit audio.** POTACAT keyed these radios over Hamlib with the command that transmits from the front microphone, so FT8 and the Station Setup test transmit keyed the radio with no output. They now key with data send, the same way WSJT-X does with "Rear/Data". Direct serial was already right. Thanks KT3H.
- **The VFO window shows only the meters your radio reports.** S, SWR, ALC and PWR rows appear once the radio has actually sent a reading for them, and a radio that reports none shows no meter section at all, instead of empty bars. POTACAT remembers what each radio reports for each connection type, so SWR, ALC and power are there from the start next time rather than appearing at the first transmission. A "No CAT control" radio shows none.
- **Auto-rotate means auto-rotate.** The rotor's quick toggle could read ON while Settings › Rotate was set to Manual, and then a spot click tuned the radio but never turned the antenna. The toggle is now called **Auto-rotate**, shows on only when tuning really turns the antenna, and switching it on sets Rotate back to Auto. The phone's and the web's rotor buttons follow the same rule. If Manual mode skips a turn, the log says so once. Thanks IK4IDF.

## Look and feel

- **macOS:** Settings and other dialogs no longer sit under the close, minimize and zoom buttons on a small window.
- **Spots table:** the frequency column is headed "Freq" instead of "Freq (kHz)". A column too narrow for its heading shows the short form ("Op" for Operator, "Call", "St") instead of a clipped "Operat…", and a NEW park pill that doesn't fit becomes "N".
- **FT8 Battle Royale removed.** The integration is no longer active, so its welcome-screen option, Settings block and JTCAT field are gone, and POTACAT no longer sends QSOs to it.
