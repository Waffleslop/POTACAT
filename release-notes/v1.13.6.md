**Headline:** On Flex Direct, POTACAT now shows the radio's own panadapter as its band scope, centred on your dial, with zoom, in the Band Scope window, under the FT8 waterfall and on your phone. Sharing your rig is now a proper loan: a guest operates under their own callsign into their own logbook, friends can take turns on your radio, and you can take it back at any time. Icom owners can help bring their radio's scope to POTACAT next by recording 30 seconds of it.

## Band scope

- **The Flex's own panadapter.** With a FlexRadio on Flex Direct, More > Band Scope shows the radio's real panadapter, not just the audio waterfall. It stays centred on the frequency you are tuned to and zooms from 5 kHz to 1 MHz with the - and + buttons. Spots are drawn on it and a click tunes. When SmartSDR is running, its panadapter is left alone.
- **The FT-710 on your phone's Pan tab.** Zoom through the radio's own spans and switch the scope to CENTER from ECHOCAT. Each phone gets frames sized for its screen and paced for its connection, so the picture is sharper on your home network and lighter over POTACAT Cloud.
- **Readings in dBm on the Flex.** The phone can read signal levels and the S-meter in dBm from a Flex panadapter.
- **Guests may zoom and centre the scope.** Your own span and mode come back when the guest's session ends or when you take the radio.
- **Icom: help us build it.** The IC-7300, IC-705, IC-7610, IC-9700 and other Icoms send their scope over the same CI-V link POTACAT already uses. Before it goes live we want recordings from real radios. Open the radio's scope screen, then go to Settings > My Rigs, edit your Icom, press **Record band scope data (30 s)** and send us the file it saves. POTACAT turns the radio's scope output on for the recording and puts it back afterwards; nothing is transmitted. On USB, 115200 baud on both the radio and POTACAT records best.

## Share my Rig

- **Guests operate as themselves.** A guest who signs in to POTACAT Cloud transmits under their own callsign in every mode you allow (FT8, CW, SSB, JS8, JTTY, SSTV, WSPR), and their contacts go to their own POTACAT Cloud logbook, never yours. Each contact also records your station as the licensee. A guest with no account can listen but not transmit.
- **Friends can take turns.** A guest can leave and come back during their pass, and the next pass holder can take the radio when it is free. A second guest who tries while someone is operating is told who has it.
- **You can take your radio back.** The Guest Pass banner in the main window has **Take over**, **Give it back** and **End now**. Taking over pauses the guest without ending their pass.
- **When a pass ends, everything stops.** FT8, Hunt and Auto CQ, WSPR, CW, SSTV and PTT all stop, and the guest is disconnected.
- **Guests bring their own voice macros.** A guest's recordings from their phone play once on your radio and are never stored.

## Radios

- **The Flex reports its real settings.** AGC, filter width, noise blanker and noise reduction, RIT, audio level, monitor, RF gain and the tuner state are read back from the radio, so every window and your phone show what the radio is really doing, even after a change in SmartSDR or on the front panel.
- **Rig presets set noise blanker and noise reduction levels** in your radio's own steps (0 to 10 on an FTX-1), and a preset that turns NB or NR off keeps it off. Thanks N0KAH.
- **SWR guard:** a bad match fixed outside POTACAT (an external tuner, or a different antenna) now clears the transmit lock once the radio reports a good SWR.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **GridTracker as your call roster.** With Settings > Logging > Also broadcast each QSO to an extra UDP port pointed at GridTracker (WSJT-X binary), **Send FT8/FT4 decodes too, and answer the station I double-click in GridTracker** fills GridTracker's call roster from the JTCAT window. A double-click there answers that station in JTCAT. Thanks AE5JE.
- **Hunt can skip stations too weak to work.** In the JTCAT window's gear menu, **Hunt: don't call below** a signal level, overall or **By band** (for example "20m -18, 6m -12"). A double-click always calls.
- **Hunt honours Skip Grid**, and Skip moves on from a station that never answered. Thanks KB2UXB.

## Logging

- **Your own state and county on every contact.** Set Home state and Home county in Settings > Station; on an activation, pick the county from the activation bar, and it is remembered for that park. LoTW and your other loggers receive it. Thanks AE5JE.
- **A park in several states takes the activator's state from their spot,** so POTACAT asks only when the spot does not say. Thanks N0KAH.
- **A reminder when your log is not backed up.** If you are signed in to POTACAT Cloud and none of the contacts on this computer have reached it, the main window says so, at most once per launch.

## ECHOCAT

- **The voice macro you rename or record elsewhere** shows straight away in an open VFO window. Thanks N0KAH.
- **The VFO window's profiles** sit in a drop-down under the dial, closed until you open it.
- **The app download badges in Settings disappear** once your own phone or tablet has connected.

## Other changes

- **The SFI, K and A numbers have left the top bar.** Solar conditions live in the Conditions window.
- **Changing the theme** in Settings now reaches the VFO, Log, pairing and Band Scope windows too.
- **Winlink:** see and change the recovery email of your Winlink account in Settings, and the log names every packet Dire Wolf hears (or says it heard none).
- **Audio devices are found again by name.** The 1.13.5 fix that re-finds a re-plugged sound card by name never loaded; it does now.
- **Station Setup's Check everything again** re-runs every free check and says so.
