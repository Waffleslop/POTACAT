**Headline:** SSTV in POTACAT is rebuilt from the ground up. Pictures decode cleanly, the SSTV window fits on one screen, replying takes two clicks, every picture says where it was heard, and your templates follow you to every computer you sign in on.

## Cleaner pictures

- **No more purple stripe:** received Martin pictures had a purple stripe down the left edge. The first few pixels of some lines were never read. They are now, and the stripe is gone.
- **Scottie keeps its red:** on busy pictures the decoder could lock onto the wrong part of a Scottie line, leaving red black and the picture's colours shifted. Scottie pictures now decode correctly, and much more cleanly when the other station's clock drifts.
- **Their call, automatically:** when the sender adds their callsign as a short tone burst after the picture (FSK ID, as MMSSTV and QSSTV do), POTACAT reads it and fills in their call for your reply.

## An SSTV window that fits

- **One screen, no scrolling:** bands, frequency, mode, Drive, SWR, power and an ATU button along the top; receive and transmit side by side; your received pictures, templates, log and packs in tabs underneath. Audio settings moved into ⚙.
- **SWR trips and the ATU:** if the SWR guard stops a picture, the SSTV window says so and offers the ATU right there.
- **Band buttons** match the FT8 window.

## Replying in two clicks

- **Double-click a received picture to reply.** Their picture goes into your reply, their call is filled in, and the reply bar keeps it all together while you pick a template. Changing templates or transmitting doesn't lose the reply.
- **Reply where they are:** every received picture shows the frequency it was heard on. If you've tuned away, or you're on the wrong sideband, the reply bar offers "Go to 14.230" in one click.
- **"UR P5" explained:** the report box takes P1–P5 as well as RSV like 595, and tells you what each means. Your reply prints whichever you used.
- **New to SSTV?** ⚙ › Help › "How SSTV contacts work" covers where to call, which sideband, modes, how a contact goes, what the reports mean, and how to identify.

## Templates with your own look

- **Nine starter templates:** CQ, three reply styles, POTA activation, QSL, QSO card, test pattern and special event, in categories you can filter.
- **Your station's own look:** fonts, colours and one of 14 backgrounds are chosen from your callsign, so your pictures look like yours. Shuffle it, or keep a look you like.
- **Save your own:** right-click the transmit picture to save it as a template. Saved templates keep their lettering even if you shuffle later.
- **Style packs:** a Halloween pack is ready to claim for the season, with more to come.
- **Templates follow you:** sign in to POTACAT Cloud and your templates, your look and your packs appear on every computer you use. ⚙ › Templates shows when they last synced. Export and Import still work without an account.
- **PD 240 and PD 290:** template text now fills the whole 640-wide picture instead of the left half.

## Logging SSTV

- **Log from the reply bar:** the log opens as SSTV with their call, the frequency and time their picture arrived, your report, and the picture mode in the comment. The cursor waits in the received-report box.
- **Log without replying:** right-click any received picture.
- **Fixed:** a pre-filled log could turn into SSB when the radio reported USB, and the Log window's clock overwrote the time a contact happened. That time now holds, which also fixes JS8Call contacts logging the wrong time.
- **40 m SSTV is LSB:** 7.171 and 7.165 were marked USB in the frequency list.

## ECHOCAT

- **The app's SSTV gallery works:** it has been empty for everyone since mid-August. It now shows your pictures, newest first, as small thumbnails that load quickly, with the full picture on tap.
- **Where each picture was heard** reaches the app too, along with the other station's FSK ID call.
- **Guest Pass:** a guest can no longer change the owner's POTACAT settings, and never sees the owner's templates.
- **ECHOCAT needs this version** for the SSTV gallery, replies with the station's call, and pack sync.

## CW and meters from ECHOCAT Web

Thanks to LZ3AW.

- **No S9+30 while transmitting** on radios where the S-meter and power readings share one command (the TS-480): the two readings no longer swap places at key-down.
- **A livelier wattmeter:** the power bar follows the radio as you transmit instead of trailing it by seconds.
- **An old ECHOCAT Web tab reloads itself** when POTACAT updates, so it can't keep running a previous version's CW code.
