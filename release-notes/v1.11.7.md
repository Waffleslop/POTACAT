**Headline:** JTTY now works the way FT8 operators expect: FT8-style messages by default, RTTY frequency manners, and it can run as your Idle Mode program. Logging is one path everywhere, so every surface writes the same records, and one contact can go into several operators' logbooks at once. CW spots show the operator's speed, a separate SDR can be your band scope on any radio, LoTW signs each activation with its own location, and a long list of fixes from LZ3AW, K9CYT, W9TEF, N0KAH, N4RDX, IK4IDF and others.

## JTTY

- **FT8-style messages by default.** The macros now follow WSJT-X 3.3's FT8-style JTTY flow: `%H %M %G`, a report, R-report, RRR, 73 and CQ, with the report measured on the other station's signal. The contest style (599 + serial) is one click away, and each style keeps its own macro list.
- **Frequency manners.** JTTY has no time slots, so a whole band sharing one offset collides. Clicking a CQ, or a station working someone else, moves you onto that station's frequency (never for a station calling you). Hold keeps you put, and **Clear spot** suggests the quietest gap from the last 30 seconds of audio.
- **JTTY as an Idle Mode program**, with a day/night marker on the band, and Idle Mode can be started and stopped from ECHOCAT.
- A TX frequency set from ECHOCAT now sticks.

## Logging

- **One way to log a contact, from every window.** The Log QSO dialog, the bottom bar, the Log QSO pop-out, the Activation logger, the Logbook's New QSO, ECHOCAT Web and FT8 auto-logging all hand the desktop what you entered, and the desktop decides the records the same way every time: one per callsign for a multi-op activation, one per park for an n-fer, your activation's parks and cross refs, each callsign with its own QRZ name, the re-spot sent once. Before this, each window built records itself and they had drifted apart.
- **Log as several operators at once.** The Log as chooser gains a "Both" (or "All") option: one contact, written into each operator's own logbook with their own callsign, QRZ upload and Cloud account.
- **A park contact is logged at the park.** The Log QSO window used the activator's home state and grid; it now uses the park's location. A park abroad takes its name and location from its spot.
- **A comma list in the Log QSO window** logs one QSO per activator. Thanks W9TEF.
- **An empty RST follows the mode**: 59 for phone, 599 for CW, -00 for digital.
- Saving a QSO no longer re-reads the whole log when no ECHOCAT device is connected. Thanks IK4IDF.
- **Logbook**: My Summit, My WWFF and My LLOTA columns.

## LoTW

- **Each activation is signed with its own grid and state.** The whole log used to be signed with your home Station Location, so park contacts either carried your home location or were refused. Home QSOs go as before, and each activation is now signed with its own park, grid and state. A park with no single state or no grid is held back and listed, never guessed.
- **QSOs LoTW already has are marked sent.** TQSL's "Previously Signed QSO" lines were counted as failures, re-sent on every upload, and listed one by one. They are now marked sent and counted as "LoTW already had", and the real skips are grouped by reason.

## Spots

- **CW speed on POTA and SOTA spots.** A park spot never says how fast the activator is sending. POTACAT now fills in the speed the Reverse Beacon Network skimmers measured on that station and frequency in the last 15 minutes, from the RBN feed, CW Spots or an RBN-style cluster node, whichever you have turned on. It shows in the WPM column and the Sync button uses it.
- **Spotter column, CQ and ITU zones** on the spots table and ECHOCAT, and **choose which DX cluster nodes feed your spots**. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **Several callsigns in the spots filter** (`LZ, SP9ABC`) and in the FT8 decode filter.
- The PSK Reporter map is capped per band, so a busy 20 m no longer pushes the other bands off.

## Band scope

- **A separate SDR as the band scope for any radio.** Point POTACAT at an rtl_tcp server (RTL-SDR, or SDRplay through rsp_tcp) in the rig editor's "Band scope from an SDR", and the band scope pop-out, the JTCAT strip and ECHOCAT's Scope tab show it, following your dial, with click-to-tune. POTACAT never opens the USB device itself. Thanks AB9AI, W9TEF and K6CSG.

## FT8 / JTCAT

- **A courtesy 73 that misses the start of its slot still goes out in that slot**, the follow-up to 1.11.6's 73 fix. Thanks IK4IDF.
- **A reply to a compound call is highlighted** in every form it arrives, bracketed or not. A Fox's compound message is placed on the map by its real sender, and a double-click calls the fox as a Hound. Thanks K9CYT.
- **Our own call is in the hash table from the first decode**, so `<K9CYT/8> W1ABC -10` resolves instead of showing `<...>`.
- **Re-log guard**: answering a repeated R-report after RR73 no longer logs the contact twice (gear menu, default 15 minutes). Thanks K9CYT.
- The activity panes resize against each other, and the map splitter sizes the PSK31 and JTTY panes too.

## CW

- **F1–F12 send CW macros 1–12** while the CW macro box is open.
- **`{greeting}`**: GM, GA or GE by the other station's local time.
- `{call}` is filled in macros sent from the ECHOCAT app. Thanks N4RDX.

## Radios

- **Flex Direct makes its own slice** when the radio restores none, instead of "radio link lost" with no reason.
- **Flex: voice and FT8 modulate with SmartSDR's DAX panel open.** POTACAT now claims the transmit stream; before, it keyed at 0 W.
- **SWR guard trips on voice into a bad match**, not only on FT8 and Tune.
- **TS-590 / TS-990 over USB** keep RTS/CTS flow control, so the link no longer drops when parked. Thanks N7UVH (#95).
- **IC-7000, IC-706MKII and IC-7300 MK II** in the Radio Model pickers.
- **Custom CAT**: a toggle can follow a radio state, and sliders take `{bcd}` for Icom CI-V levels.
- **Clock "Fix it"** steps the clock by the measured offset and measures again.

## VFO window

- **Redesigned**: reorder its sections, an SDR menu that fits on screen (with "Add new SDR"), and an SWR card.
- A quick PTT press no longer leaves a mic tap running or keys after release.
- LOG fills the call and park of the spot the VFO card shows.

## ECHOCAT

- **ECHOCAT Web**: FT8 band follows a spot click, Logbook filter bar, a Prop view (RBN and PSK Reporter), the PSK31 pane, DX Expeditions in the Spots menu, F-key macros, a Live button for CW, readable greys, the FT8 period bar on the desktop's clock, and tapping a spot's callsign opens QRZ. Thanks LZ3AW.
- **The watchlist syncs** between the desktop and ECHOCAT devices.
- TX power and RF gain set from a device are confirmed by the radio, and a TX power the radio has not reported yet is no longer shown as 0 W. Thanks N4RDX.
- Every re-spot template (WWFF, LLOTA, WWBOTA too) and your saved re-spot messages reach the device. Thanks K8IKO.
- A web page that was displaced by another device ends its audio call.
