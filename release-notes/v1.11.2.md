**Headline:** Hide or skip a spot on one device and it's hidden or skipped everywhere, a bouncing paddle no longer sends extra dots and dashes, and the VFO window shows the right activator from the moment it opens.

## Spots

- **Hide and skip follow you:** hiding or skipping a spot in POTACAT, ECHOCAT Web or the ECHOCAT app now does the same on the others. Hides you already had move over. Skips are still a "not now": they clear at 0000Z. Thanks K8IKO.
- **"Hide forever" stays hidden:** a spot hidden forever on the desktop came back after a restart. It no longer does.
- **Worked spots gray out:** a spot reported as USB didn't gray out after you logged the QSO as SSB. It does now. Thanks KC1SSY.
- **ECHOCAT Web marks today's contacts:** a station you logged at the desktop now dims in ECHOCAT Web even when your log is too large to send in full. Thanks K8IKO.

## VFO window

- **The activator shows at once:** opening the VFO window while on a spot used to show nothing until you clicked another row. Thanks N0KAH.
- **Only the stations the spot list shows:** the VFO no longer lists expired spots on the same frequency, and with two or three operators each line shows their park and where the spot came from.

## CW

- **No more extra dots and dashes:** a paddle contact that bounces as it closes counted as a second press, so one tap could send two dots or two dashes, in Iambic A and B alike. Both the ECHOCAT Web keyer and POTACAT's own keyer now ignore the bounce. Thanks LZ3AW.

## Mac

- **One set of window buttons:** the Log, VFO, Spots, DX Cluster, JS8, SSTV and Mercury windows showed their own buttons next to the Mac's, with the title under them. They now use the Mac's only. Thanks N0KAH.

## Fixes

- **FT-710 band scope keeps up with the radio:** after 20 minutes without touching POTACAT (for example, talking on the radio), the scope, VFO and log line could stay on the old frequency after you changed bands. POTACAT no longer pauses reading the radio while the band scope is open. Thanks N8VCL.
- **Clock check:** FT8's clock check could report a correctly synced clock as off. It now trusts its fastest time servers, and when it does warn, the log lists what each server said. Thanks KC1SSY.
