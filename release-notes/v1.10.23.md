We asked on Discord what you wanted fixed. This release is the answer, plus a setup checklist for every radio and a Hamlib rotor backend.

## 🧰 Station Setup: one checklist per radio

**More → Station Setup** (also in the Settings sidebar, and it opens by itself after Add Rig). One list per radio, and a green tick only when POTACAT checked it:

- Your callsign and grid
- Radio control
- **Hearing the radio**: a 3-second listen on the radio's audio input
- **Sending audio to the radio**: the output is opened the way FT8 opens it
- **Test transmit**: after a warning in the window, POTACAT drops to 5 W (or the radio's maximum if lower), sends a 1000 Hz tone for 3 seconds, measures power and SWR, and puts your power back. It only counts the restore as done when the radio reports the new setting.
- Your PC clock
- CW keying, only for radios with a confirmed menu setting
- FT-710 band scope: **Find the file for me** installs FTDI's LibFT4222 (the 64-bit copy from the `amd64` folder) with no admin rights, and it survives updates.

If an essential step has never worked on a radio, a card offers the checklist 12 seconds after launch. A radio that is simply switched off never triggers it. "Don't ask again" is per radio. FT-710 owners get a one-time "new for your radio" card for the band scope.

**Share my working setup.** Once every essential step works, the checklist offers to send that radio's working settings, so they can become the defaults for everyone with the same radio:
- You see everything that will be sent, in plain words, before you press Send.
- No passwords, addresses or device IDs are ever sent. Your callsign goes only if you tick the box. Device names that contain a person's name (like "Casey's AirPods") are left out.
- Details are in the updated [privacy policy](https://potacat.com/privacy-terms.html#setup-sharing).

## 🔄 Rotor control through Hamlib

A third rotor option beside PstRotator and Rotor-EZ: **Hamlib rotctld**, which covers every rotator Hamlib supports (GS-232, SPID, Easycomm, ARS, Prosistel and more), at no cost. POTACAT can start rotctld for you, bound to this computer only, or connect to one you already run. It reads the rotor's real limits, so a south-centred rotor turns the right way for a west bearing. Thanks KD9WI.

## 🍻 What you asked for

- **Activation logs are complete** (N7VBN). Contacts you hunt while activating now carry MY_GRIDSQUARE, OPERATOR and STATION_CALLSIGN, and every activation contact gets MY_STATE when the park is in a single state. Contacts logged on the Act screen with no frequency were exported as `FREQ NaN` with no band. POTACAT now asks for the frequency instead, and never writes NaN. FT8 activation contacts no longer carry your home grid. **If you have `NaN` entries from an earlier activation, add the frequency to those contacts before uploading.**
- **Light mode is readable in the sun** (Dusty Notes). White text on red throughout. The FT8 "called me" row had dark red text on a red bar. The light theme's red is darker.
- **The band scope window crops instead of squashing** (Scott). Making it smaller now drops the oldest rows at the same scale.
- **CW auto-sync on the web** (KE4WLE). Press **Sync** once to match the spotted station's speed. Double-press or hold to turn on auto-sync, and the speed follows whatever spotted CW station you're tuned to, in CW mode only. POTA spots now keep the WPM that RBN heard, so there's usually a speed to match.
- **Hear your voice macros** (KW4FM). A **HEAR** button beside REC plays a macro on your computer while it transmits. On the web interface it's a checkbox under Voice Macros. It's off by default; use headphones if your radio also picks up its microphone.
- **The Remote Launcher starts POTACAT for real** (lor, GitHub #84, KB2UXB):
  - Start used to say "started" while POTACAT exited at once.
  - After a reboot, the launcher's own processes counted as "POTACAT is running", so Start did nothing.
  - Start now waits to confirm POTACAT is up.
  - An installed launcher is refreshed on every update, so older launcher fixes finally reach you.
  - On Linux it now finds your settings, the running app and deb/rpm installs.

## 🌐 ECHOCAT web (LZ3AW, round 6)

- **Spots menu and filters.** The second tap no longer throws an error, and the band, mode, region and source filters work again.
- **Full worked history on the web.** "Worked history too large … today only" is gone; the whole history now arrives in pieces.
- **Paddle.** A covered or background browser window no longer cuts a held paddle after a few seconds. The server's keyer now uses your paddle-swap and iambic-mode settings, so the local sidetone matches what the radio sends.
- **Custom sliders** keep the value you set.
- **Power meters.**
  - The VFO window's bar scales to your power setting instead of sticking at full.
  - After CW, an S-meter reading no longer shows up as watts.
  - The raw meter value is now logged, for calibrating the TS-480 reading.

## 📻 Transmit fixes

- **Tune on a Flex puts out power.** Tune in the FT8 window now sends through the same path as FT8, at FT8's audio level and your TX audio offset, as WSJT-X does. It measured 0 W on a Flex 8600 whose FT8 made 53 W.
- **Tune refuses a missing audio output.** If your saved rig output is gone, Tune stops and says so instead of keying a silent carrier for 90 seconds. Voice macros now do the same.
- **The Settings Save button keeps your rig edits.** Typing in the rig editor and pressing the main Save used to throw the edit away, which is why a status-pill label like "FLEX" never stuck.
- **One waterfall bar in the FT8 window** when RX and TX are on the same frequency, and calling CQ moves RX back to your own frequency.

## 🔧 Other fixes

- **FT-710 / rigctld:** Test Connection waits for the COM port to be released, and says "in use" rather than "does not exist" (K5AWJ).
- **JS8 on USB-audio radios:** it could run with nothing listening to the audio. It now captures (KN4IIG).
- **Mode hop:**
  - It now says why it's waiting.
  - Calling CQ counts as attended operation.
  - The rotation chips no longer use Field Day's orange (N2FSM).
- **Propagation view:**
  - Ages keep counting instead of freezing at the last update.
  - An empty table says why: nothing heard, PSKReporter unavailable, or everything filtered out.
- **SWR guard:** an FT8 QSO held behind a tripped SWR guard is abandoned instead of sitting for hours and blocking Hunt.
