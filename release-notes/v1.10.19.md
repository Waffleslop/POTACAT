A short release about things that stopped part way and said nothing. A QSO logged from the pop-out now reaches your external logbook, a held paddle keeps keying, the wattmeter has a reading on every radio, and the toolbar on a mobile device stops freezing.

## 📒 QSOs logged from the pop-out reach your logbook

**W9TEF** logged a contact, watched it land in the POTACAT Logbook, and watched it never arrive in World Radio League. Finding the same contact in the logbook and clicking **Resend to Logbook** delivered it instantly. He reproduced that on three versions in a row.

The difference between the two paths was the transmit power field. The Log QSO pop-out sent it as a number while every other part of POTACAT sends it as text, and the code that builds the logbook packet did text work on it. It failed before anything was sent. The resend worked because it rebuilds the contact from your ADIF file, where the field is already text.

- **A QSO logged from the Log QSO pop-out now reaches WRL.** Nothing about the callsign mattered — the same failure happened with a test call and with his own.
- **The same fault would have hit HamRS, Log4OM in binary mode, MacLoggerDX and Logger32.** Those use a different sender that fails the same way on a number. Nobody had reported it; W9TEF simply happened to be using WRL. It is fixed too.
- **Power is now accepted whatever form it arrives in,** at the point each logbook packet is built, so one unusual value can no longer cost you a contact.

## 📻 The wattmeter has a reading on every radio

1.10.18 added a transmit power bar to the main window, the VFO and JTCAT pop-outs and ECHOCAT. **LZ3AW** reported back: "there is a bargraph, but not moving."

The reading was being taken from the radio and then going nowhere. Only Flex radios ever had a working source. **Every direct serial and Hamlib radio now shows measured power.** If your radio answers no CAT at all while transmitting, POTACAT says so once in the log, because on such a link the meter cannot work and silence looked like a fault.

**On ECHOCAT Web the bar was hidden behind a checkbox labelled "Show S-Meter / SWR".** It names every meter in the strip now.

## ⌨️ A held paddle keeps keying

Also from **LZ3AW**: the keyer still stopped a second or two into a long squeeze, even after the 1.10.18 fix for the same symptom. That fix was real, but this was a second, separate cause in ECHOCAT.

- **A held contact no longer stops after a second and a half.** ECHOCAT's safety check expected a steady stream of messages and a held paddle sends exactly one. Clients now repeat a held contact, and clients that do not, including the mobile app and older builds, are covered from the keyer itself.
- **The radio is no longer left marked as transmitting after you stop.** Each paddle movement added its full safety margin to a running total, so half a minute of sending could leave POTACAT believing the radio was still transmitting minutes later — holding PTT, and skipping the check that notices a radio has actually gone away.

## 📱 The toolbar on a mobile device stops freezing

On ECHOCAT Web on a phone or tablet, opening a dropdown in the spot toolbar scrolled the bar back to the start under your finger. The button you had just tapped slid away, often off screen and taking the panel with it, and the bar stayed stuck until something closed the panel: "push it does nothing and the whole bar line stucks."

**The toolbar is no longer touched when a panel opens.** The bar grew past a phone's width when the callsign filter joined it in 1.10.18, which is what made this reachable.

## 🎛️ Custom CAT sliders in the VFO windows

**Custom CAT slider buttons are now drawn in the VFO pop-out and the ECHOCAT Web VFO panel,** not just the main window. Only custom buttons appeared there before, so a slider you had set up was missing wherever you actually operate from.

## 🎯 ECHOCAT Web answers special-event callsigns

A station decoding as `CQ <SP9ABC/P>` was the one station ECHOCAT Web would not answer in Hunt. The desktop learned to read the brackets a decoder puts around a resolved callsign in 1.10.18; the web client kept its own older reader. **They now share one.**
