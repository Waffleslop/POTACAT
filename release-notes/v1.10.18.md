Most of this release is about things that failed with nothing on screen to say so. Your logbook and the cloud now agree, CW on the Kenwood TS-480 keeps transmitting, JTCAT Hunt answers on the right slot, and SSTV decodes quiet sound cards. There's also a callsign filter for the spot list and an optional chime when a QSO is logged.

## ☁️ Your log and the cloud now agree

The admin page showed some synced QSOs with a blank callsign, date and frequency. Some QSOs never reached the cloud at all. Both came from the same root cause: every way of changing the log had to remember to tell the cloud, and several didn't.

- **Every change to your log now syncs.** That covers edits and deletes from ECHOCAT, activation deletes, activator edits, event stamps, LoTW flags and imports. Before, all of these could change your log and never reach the cloud.
- **Changes sync whenever you're signed in.** Syncing used to depend on the auto-sync checkbox, which is off by default. With it off, changes were dropped and Sync Now still said it succeeded. Sync Now now says what it sent.
- **Damaged records are repaired once, with a backup.** Some QSOs were stored with the wrong field names. POTACAT couldn't read them back, so each pull added another copy. One log held a single QSO 108 times. POTACAT fixes the names on disk and removes the exact duplicates. It writes a backup of your log first.
- **Signing in uploads anything the cloud is missing.** That includes QSOs logged while you were signed out and imports from before imports synced. A QSO you deleted on another device stays deleted.
- **Shared shack PCs keep each operator's QSOs separate.** Whoever signed in next used to upload the previous operator's contacts into their own cloud logbook. Each account now uploads only its own. The Cloud tab shows how many are waiting for another account.
- **A second edit before a sync is no longer thrown away.** It used to reach the server marked as a version it already had, and was dropped.
- **Imports skip contacts you already have.** POTACAT matches on the callsign, the same kind of mode, within 5 kHz and within 3 minutes. The logbook also has a new **Find Duplicates** button for copies that are already there.
- **Each park in a multi-park QSO gets its own cloud record.** They used to overwrite a single one.

## 🌐 Cloud Tunnel: "Live" now means it really works

**K5AWJ**'s tunnel was healthy for hours while his `callsign.potacat.com` page returned a Cloudflare error. POTACAT itself wasn't answering behind the tunnel, and the tunnel card still said Live.

- **The Cloud tab checks that POTACAT answers,** the same way Cloudflare does. It only shows Live and a clickable link when it does. Otherwise it says why. The possible reasons are that the ECHOCAT port was changed from 7300 (the tunnel always uses 7300), that the port is in use by another program, or that the security certificate couldn't be created.
- **A busy port keeps retrying** every 30 seconds instead of giving up after a few tries, and a notice stays up until it's solved. Before, the first retry could crash POTACAT.
- **Bug reports include the ECHOCAT server's state.**

## 🚀 Please read: starting at login is now your choice

Every installed copy of POTACAT used to add the **Remote Launcher** to your computer's login items on first run, without telling you. **It is now added only when you click Install** in Settings > ECHOCAT. If you already installed it, it stays installed.

If the Cloud Tunnel is on and POTACAT doesn't start at login, the Cloud tab now offers to start it at login. Without that, nothing reconnects after your computer restarts until you open POTACAT. **Not now** hides the offer until the next launch.

## 📻 CW on the Kenwood TS-480

**LZ3AW** tested round after round of these on his TS-480, and sent the bug report that showed where the problem really was.

- **The radio keeps transmitting.** The TS-480 stops answering CAT while it transmits. After ten seconds of CW, POTACAT decided the radio had disconnected and stopped keying it. POTACAT now knows the radio is transmitting for as long as the message, the key-as-you-type text or the paddle is keying.
- **Key-as-you-type on a CW key port works at full speed.** Each new letter used to cut off the one before it, so slow typing worked and fast typing didn't. Letters now queue.
- **The paddle no longer stops after a few seconds.** That was the same disconnect check.
- **SSB filter width now changes the radio.** On a TS-480, SSB width is set with the high and low cut. POTACAT used a filter command that can only pick between the radio's fitted filters. The high cut now follows your width, your low cut stays where you set it, and the width shown is what the radio actually has.
- **The wattmeter shows transmit power.** The reading was being overwritten by the power setting and wasn't shown anywhere. The main window, the VFO and JTCAT pop-outs and ECHOCAT now show it.

## ⌨️ `{call}` in CW macros

`{call}` is now **the callsign you typed most recently, in any field, on the desktop or ECHOCAT Web**. That covers the log dialog, the Log pop-out, the activator quick log, the banner logger, and the web log sheet, quick log, Log tab and VFO panel. Before, an old callsign in another box could win. The VFO pop-out's free-text box now fills in `{call}` too.

## ✅ Worked marks, per band and mode

**The check mark now means worked on this band and mode.** A station you worked on 40 m CW is a new contact on 20 m SSB. A spot's SSB now matches your logged USB or LSB, and FT4 matches FT4 however your logger wrote it.

## 🌐 ECHOCAT Web

- **The activator quick log follows your radio.** It used to fill in the frequency once and not change again until you refreshed the page. It stops following if you type your own frequency.
- **Custom CAT toggle buttons work.** You couldn't type the Off command in a browser, so every toggle was saved with an empty Off. The VFO pop-out on the desktop now alternates On and Off too.
- **The Log tab takes a UTC date and time** for logging a past QSO. The desktop New QSO form does too, with today's date filled in.

## 🔎 New: filter spots by callsign

There's a new box in the spot toolbar, on the desktop and ECHOCAT Web (**LZ3AW**'s request). Enter callsigns or prefixes separated by commas or spaces, such as `LZ, SP9ABC`, and only matching spots are shown. `*` and `?` work as wildcards.

## 🔔 New: a chime for every logged QSO

**N2FSM** asked for a way to know a contact was logged when POTACAT is in the background. Settings > Spots has a new **QSO chime** option, next to the notification settings: Off (the default), Soft ding, Two-tone, Bell or Morse R, with a Test button. It plays for every logged QSO, from the log dialog, quick log, JTCAT, ECHOCAT or WSJT-X.

## 🎯 JTCAT Hunt answers on the right slot

**K4HXM**: "Hunt POTA will often TX on the same time slot as the CQ POTA. Not always, just often." Each decode knew which slot it was heard in, but Hunt never received that. It assumed even and always replied on odd. That collided with every activator calling on odd, which is why it happened often but not always. Hunt now replies on the slot opposite the one the activator used.

**A CQ from a special-event call in brackets** (`CQ <SP9ABC/P>`) now answers when you double-click it. It used to do nothing.

## 🖼️ SSTV hears quiet sound cards

**WB8IMY** could see strong SSTV images on the waterfall while the picture stayed black. MMSSTV decoded the same audio fine. His sound card sent the audio about 40 dB below full scale. POTACAT's decoder needed it to be louder, and the waterfall adjusts itself, so it looked strong anyway.

- **The decoder now raises quiet audio before decoding.** In testing it decoded the same picture at every level down to 60 dB below full scale. Audio that is already loud enough is decoded exactly as before.
- **The SSTV window shows your audio level** next to Audio In, and a yellow **RX AUDIO LOW** badge appears when it's too quiet to rely on. The decode log shows the level as `in=` too.

## 🔧 Also in this release

- **Hunt in Activator mode always shows the spots table.** If SWL/HF Nets or another view was open before, it used to come back instead of the spots.
