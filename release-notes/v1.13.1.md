**Headline:** Winlink email is in POTACAT. Send and receive mail from the shack over the internet on Windows, Linux and Mac, and over the radio with ARDOP on Windows and Linux, with nothing else to install. Also: N1MM+ can drive your radio through POTACAT (even a remote shack), Yaesu SWR now reads the SWR meter instead of ALC, and the radio's audio is never taken from the PC's microphone.

## Winlink email

- **Mail from your shack.** More > **Winlink Mail** opens a mail window with Inbox, Outbox, Sent and Archive, a composer with attachments, and a position report. POTACAT runs the open-source Pat client in the background; there is nothing to install or turn on.
- **New to Winlink?** Settings > Station > **Winlink (email over radio)** > **Check my account** tells you whether your callsign has an account and, if not, **Create my Winlink account** makes one with the password and recovery email you choose. If you already have one, type its password there and save.
- **Over the internet** (Windows, Linux and Mac): pick Internet in Send via and press Send / Receive. There is also an optional "check mail over the internet every N minutes". It never uses the radio.
- **Over the radio with ARDOP** (Windows and Linux): pick ARDOP, choose a band, and pick a gateway from the list (nearest first). The radio tunes there at once and a waterfall with a **Busy / Clear** badge shows whether the frequency is in use, then Send / Receive. POTACAT keys the radio, moves it to data mode, puts the dial back when the session ends, and stops a session that stalls or runs past your time limit. Power, PWR and SWR sit on the session line, and the SWR guard stops a session into a bad match.
- **No sound card setup on a Flex (Flex Direct) or an Icom over the network:** the modem's audio rides POTACAT's own link to the radio, so DAX is not needed. On a radio with a USB sound card, POTACAT picks the same devices your FT8 uses and only asks when it cannot tell.
- **Packet** (VHF/UHF Winlink through the built-in Dire Wolf) is also there on Windows and Linux.
- **From your phone or a browser:** ECHOCAT Web has a Mail tab with the same folders, composer, gateway list, waterfall and Send / Receive; the shack holds the mailbox and the radio. The ECHOCAT app's Mail tab arrives in an app update.
- Winlink is amateur radio: the first message or session shows a short notice (no business traffic, nothing that needs privacy). The Winlink network is run by the Amateur Radio Safety Foundation.

## Radios

- **N1MM+ (or another logger) can control the radio.** Settings > Tuning > **Let N1MM+ (or another logger) control the radio**. POTACAT becomes a Kenwood TS-2000 on this PC: in N1MM+, Config > Configure Ports, Port TCP, Radio TS-2000, 127.0.0.1 and port 4535. Frequency, band changes, mode, PTT and CW go through POTACAT, so N1MM on a laptop that is driving your remote shack works the shack's radio. Thanks NK8Q.
- **Yaesu SWR was the ALC meter.** On the FT-991A, FTDX10, FT-710, FT-891, FTDX101 and other Yaesus, POTACAT read the ALC meter as SWR, so a perfect match could show 3:1. It now reads the SWR meter, scaled the way Hamlib and WSJT-X scale it. Icom SWR readings are scaled the same way and now match the radio's own meter.
- **Sound-card PTT for radios with no CAT** (Digirig Lite, CM108/CM119 cables). In a "No CAT control (VOX)" radio, choose **The USB sound card keys it** under Transmit (PTT), and a Baofeng or similar keys from the desktop, ECHOCAT or FT8.
- **FT-710:** a rig preset's **Use current** now reads RF gain and AF gain from the radio. Thanks W3MDT.
- **IC-9700 over the network:** the VFO window and phone PTT now send your voice to the radio over the LAN (they played your own mic on the PC's speakers and sent nothing), the speaker button plays the radio, and a band click works when the sub receiver already holds that band. Thanks KG5EIU.
- **FT-817/818, FT-857 and FT-897** now speak Yaesu's own CAT: mode, tuning and PTT work, and the link no longer drops every few minutes. Intel Macs also get Intel builds of the radio helpers. Thanks KL4XQ.

## ECHOCAT

- **The radio's audio is never the PC's microphone.** If the radio's input setting went missing, ECHOCAT and FT8 used to open the PC's default microphone, so a guest could hear the room and FT8 decoded only with the radio's speaker turned up. POTACAT now restores the radio's own sound card, and never falls back to a microphone. Thanks KF0WWL.
- **Password-protect your rig** is one setting, directly under the ECHOCAT addresses (Settings > ECHOCAT). The web page asks for it; the app gets it when it pairs.
- **ECHOCAT Web FT8 transmits after a second spot click** or a typed frequency. Before, the first spot worked and the next one started the QSO but never keyed. Thanks LZ3AW.
- Dropdown menus on the web page no longer close when you scroll to the end of them, and FT4/FT2 rows and CW spot rows line up again. Thanks LZ3AW.

## Spots and logging

- **Worked today on another band.** A spot you have not worked on its band and mode, but did work today on another, shows a dim checkmark; hover it to see which band and mode. The full checkmark still means worked on this band and mode, because another band is another contact.
- **The Log QSO form moves.** Drag it by its title bar off the callsigns you are logging; it opens where you left it. Double-click the title to centre it. Thanks N3CDF.
- **Ctrl+L brings the tuned spot's park.** The Log window now opens as POTA (or SOTA, WWFF) with the reference filled in, like the Log button. Thanks N0KAH.
- **Changing the callsign in the Log form looks up that operator,** so the second op at a multi-op activation gets their own name from QRZ.

## SSTV

- **Keep a picture.** A picture decoded from a weak signal is shown but not saved on its own; it now has a **Keep** button that saves it to Received, so it is there to reply to.
